> [!abstract] Thiel Fellowship
> Launched 2010: $100,000 over two years to people under 23 to drop out of university and
> build something instead. Deliberately framed as an attack on higher education.

## Key facts
- Run through the [[Thiel Foundation]]; [[Blake Masters]] presided over it during its most productive years, with Danielle Strachman and Mike Gibson running operations.
- Notable fellows: [[Vitalik Buterin]] (Ethereum), [[Dylan Field]] (Figma), [[Austin Russell]] (Luminar), Laura Deming (longevity), Paul Gu (Upstart), Ritesh Agarwal (OYO).
- Widely criticised at launch — Larry Summers called it the single most misdirected philanthropy of the decade — and widely imitated since.
- Functions as a recruiting funnel: fellows and their companies show up repeatedly in [[Founders Fund]]'s portfolio and in the network's younger cohort, e.g. [[Delian Asparouhov]].

## Connections
