> [!abstract] Gigafund
> Fund founded 2017 by [[Luke Nosek]] and Stephen Oskoui after Nosek left [[Founders Fund]],
> built around a highly concentrated position in [[SpaceX]].

## Key facts
- Explicitly designed for very long holding periods in a small number of companies — a rejection of standard venture portfolio construction.
- Has raised multiple vehicles primarily to buy SpaceX secondaries.
- Also backs Boring Company and other Musk-adjacent ventures.

## Connections
- [[Luke Nosek]] — co-founder.
- [[SpaceX]], [[Elon Musk]] — the concentration.
- [[Founders Fund]] — his previous firm.
