> [!abstract] Office of Personnel Management
> The federal government's HR agency — the lever for reshaping the civil service — directed since 2025 by [[Andreessen Horowitz]]'s former managing partner [[Scott Kupor]].

## Key facts
- Ran the "Fork in the Road" deferred-resignation offer and the mass reduction-in-force machinery during 2025.
- Its systems were among the first the [[Department of Government Efficiency]] obtained access to, producing privacy litigation.
- Reclassifying and replacing career civil servants — the Project 2025 proposal [[Russ Vought]] executes — runs through this agency.
- Puts the operational centre of the civil-service argument [[Curtis Yarvin]] has made for years in the hands of a venture capitalist.
