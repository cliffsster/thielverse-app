> [!abstract] American Civil Liberties Union
> The most persistent institutional opponent of this network's government work — and one of
> the few nodes in this graph pulling in the opposite direction.

## Key facts
- Has litigated and filed FOIA requests for years over [[Palantir Technologies]]' systems at [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation]], forcing much of what is publicly known about those contracts into the open.
- Documents and challenges automated licence-plate-reader networks including [[Flock Safety]], and border surveillance including [[Anduril Industries]]' towers.
- Raised the central objection to 2025 federal data consolidation at the [[Internal Revenue Service]] and elsewhere: that removing compartmentalisation between government systems is a constitutional change made by procurement.

## Connections
- [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Flock Safety]], [[Anduril Industries]] — litigation and documentation targets.
- [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], [[U.S. Department of Homeland Security]], [[Internal Revenue Service]].
- [[Electronic Frontier Foundation]], [[Mijente]] — allied opposition.
