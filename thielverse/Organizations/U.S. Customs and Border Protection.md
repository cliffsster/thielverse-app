> [!abstract] U.S. Customs and Border Protection
> [[Anduril Industries]]' founding customer — the autonomous surveillance towers that proved
> the company could sell hardware to the US government without a prime contractor.

## Key facts
- Anduril's Sentry towers were first deployed on the southern border in 2018 and have expanded across multiple sectors under CBP's Autonomous Surveillance Tower programme.
- The towers use Anduril's Lattice software to detect and classify movement without continuous human monitoring — the design [[Palmer Luckey]] built the company around.
- A [[Flock Safety]] pilot with CBP was ended in 2025 after public backlash over licence-plate data sharing.
