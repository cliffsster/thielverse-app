> [!abstract] SpaceX
> Founded 2002 by [[Elon Musk]] with his [[PayPal]] proceeds. [[Founders Fund]]'s investment,
> championed by [[Luke Nosek]], is one of the highest-returning venture positions in history.

## Key facts
- Musk's ~$180m from the eBay sale was the seed capital; without PayPal there is no SpaceX.
- Founders Fund invested in 2008 when almost no institutional investor would touch launch vehicles — the same contrarian defence-adjacent stance that later produced [[Anduril Industries]].
- [[Luke Nosek]] left Founders Fund in 2017 to start [[Gigafund]], concentrated heavily in SpaceX.
- [[Cyan Banister]] made an early personal investment.
- Starlink and Starshield now make SpaceX a direct national-security contractor, putting it in the same customer set as [[Palantir Technologies]] and Anduril.

## Connections
- [[Elon Musk]] — founder.
- [[Luke Nosek]], [[Founders Fund]], [[Gigafund]], [[Cyan Banister]] — capital.
- [[U.S. Department of Defense]] — launch and Starshield customer.
