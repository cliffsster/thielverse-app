> [!abstract] Stanford University
> The physical origin point for most of this network's relationships.

## Key facts
- [[Peter Thiel]]: BA philosophy 1989, JD 1992 — where he met [[Reid Hoffman]] (symbolic systems) and [[Alex Karp]] (law school).
- [[David Sacks]] and [[Keith Rabois]]: undergraduates who wrote for the [[Stanford Review]].
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] and [[Stephen Cohen]]: computer science, where they met and later co-founded [[Palantir Technologies]].
- [[Blake Masters]]: BA and JD; his notes from Thiel's CS183 course became *Zero to One*.
- [[Roelof Botha]]: MBA. [[Premal Shah]]: economics. [[Jawed Karim]]: graduate study after [[YouTube]].
- The [[Thiel Fellowship]] was designed in explicit opposition to the institution that produced all of this.
