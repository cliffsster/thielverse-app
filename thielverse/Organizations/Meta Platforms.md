> [!abstract] Meta Platforms (Facebook)
> [[Peter Thiel]]'s $500,000 in August 2004 bought 10.2% and made him a billionaire; he sat
> on the board until 2022.

## Key facts
- [[Sean Parker]], then Facebook's president, brokered the Thiel introduction to [[Mark Zuckerberg]].
- [[Reid Hoffman]] also invested in the same early round — two future political opponents on the same cap table.
- Acquired Oculus in 2014, then fired [[Palmer Luckey]] in 2017 over a political donation; Luckey founded [[Anduril Industries]] months later.
- Employed [[Yishan Wong]] and other [[PayPal]] engineers during its scaling years.
