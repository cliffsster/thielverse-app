> [!abstract] PayPal
> The company that produced this entire network. Formed from the 2000 merger of
> [[Confinity]] and [[X.com]]; sold to eBay for $1.5B in October 2002.

## Key facts
- The "PayPal Mafia" label came from a 2007 *Fortune* article and its staged photo of alumni in mob costume.
- Roughly two dozen people left with meaningful wealth in their late 20s and early 30s, plus a shared operating culture and a habit of backing each other.
- Its defining technical problem was fraud at scale — solved by [[Max Levchin]], [[Nathan Gettings]] and [[David Gausebeck]], and later commercialised as [[Palantir Technologies]].
- Leadership churn was brutal: [[Bill Harris]] out in 2000, [[Elon Musk]] out in September 2000, [[Peter Thiel]] in as CEO.
