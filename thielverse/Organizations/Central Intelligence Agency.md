> [!abstract] Central Intelligence Agency
> [[Palantir Technologies]]' first customer, and via [[In-Q-Tel]] its first institutional
> investor.

## Key facts
- In-Q-Tel, the CIA's venture arm founded in 1999 under [[Gilman Louie]], put ~$2m into Palantir around 2005.
- More valuable than the money: CIA analysts worked with Palantir engineers for roughly three years, shaping Gotham into something intelligence analysts would actually use.
- That relationship gave Palantir the credibility to sell across the rest of the intelligence and defence community.
