> [!abstract] 8VC
> Venture firm founded 2015 by [[Joe Lonsdale]], successor to his earlier Formation 8.
> Moved to Austin in 2020.

## Key facts
- Invests across healthcare, logistics, defence and government software — an extension of the [[Palantir Technologies]] thesis into new verticals.
- Portfolio includes [[Addepar]], [[OpenGov]], Anduril-adjacent defence companies, Blend, Wish and Synthego.
- [[Alex Moore]], an early Palantir employee, is a partner.
- The firm is the financial engine behind Lonsdale's broader institution-building — the [[Cicero Institute]] and the [[University of Austin]].

## Connections
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — founder.

- [[Addepar]], [[OpenGov]], [[Cicero Institute]], [[University of Austin]] — the Lonsdale cluster.
- [[Founders Fund]] — parallel network fund, frequent co-investor.
