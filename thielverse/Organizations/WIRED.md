> [!abstract] WIRED
> Broke much of the 2025 reporting on [[Department of Government Efficiency]] operations and on [[Palantir Technologies]]' work consolidating federal data.

## Key facts
- Reported extensively on DOGE's access to Treasury, OPM and agency payment systems, and on the young engineers deployed across departments.
- Published the reporting that Palantir engineers were building a consolidated API over [[Internal Revenue Service]] taxpayer data.
- Covered the Social Security and HHS data-consolidation efforts in the same period.
- Alongside [[404 Media]], carried the load on the 2025 surveillance story that legacy outlets came to later.
