> [!abstract] NATO
> Acquired [[Palantir Technologies]]' Maven Smart System in 2025 — one of the fastest
> procurements in the alliance's history and the clearest sign the company's model has been
> exported beyond the US.

## Key facts
- The Maven Smart System applies AI to target identification and operational planning; NATO's Allied Command Operations acquired it in spring 2025.
- The speed of the purchase — reportedly a matter of months — was itself the point, and was cited by NATO officials as a deliberate break from normal acquisition timelines.
- Extends the pattern already visible in the [[Israel Ministry of Defense]] partnership and Palantir's Ukraine work: the company now sells sovereign-scale capability to allied states, not just the US.
