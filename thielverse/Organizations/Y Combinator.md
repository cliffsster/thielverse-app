> [!abstract] Y Combinator
> Startup accelerator founded 2005 by [[Paul Graham]], Jessica Livingston, Trevor Blackwell
> and Robert Morris. Run by [[Sam Altman]] 2014–2019, and by [[Garry Tan]] since 2023.

## Key facts
- Funded Airbnb, Stripe, Dropbox, Coinbase, DoorDash, Instacart and [[Flock Safety]]-era security startups; the standard entry point into Silicon Valley for founders without a network.
- [[Peter Thiel]] joined as a part-time partner in 2015 under Altman — a brief but real formal link between the Thiel apparatus and YC.
- Altman ran Hydrazine Capital, whose largest backer was Thiel, and used it to invest in YC companies while president.
- Altman left in 2019 for [[OpenAI]], which YC had incubated support for; [[Paul Graham]] later said Altman had been asked to choose between the two.
- Under Tan, YC has become an active lobbying presence in Washington, aligned with [[Marc Andreessen]]'s "Little Tech" agenda against AI regulation.

## Connections
- [[Sam Altman]], [[Paul Graham]], [[Garry Tan]] — successive leadership.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — former part-time partner and Hydrazine backer.
- [[OpenAI]] — Altman's next move.
- [[Andreessen Horowitz]] — aligned on AI policy.
