> [!abstract] National Security Agency
> Signals-intelligence agency; part of the intelligence-community customer base that
> [[Palantir Technologies]] entered after [[In-Q-Tel]] validated it.

## Key facts
- Documents published after the 2013 Snowden disclosures showed Palantir software in use across allied signals-intelligence agencies as an analysis layer over bulk collection.
- Palantir's public position has always been that it builds access controls and audit logs, not collection systems — a distinction critics call cosmetic.
- The NSA's bulk-collection programmes are the political context that made [[John Poindexter]]'s Total Information Awareness lineage a live controversy for Palantir throughout the 2010s.
