> [!abstract] AIPAC
> The American Israel Public Affairs Committee. Present in this graph as **a pole in the
> right's civil war**, not as a node with ties to the Thiel network.

## Key facts
- Founded 1963; the principal pro-Israel lobbying organisation in US politics.
- Its affiliated super PAC, the United Democracy Project, spent heavily from 2022 onward in Democratic primaries and was central to defeating several incumbent members of Congress in 2024.
- Its relevance here is oppositional: [[Tucker Carlson]], [[Nick Fuentes]] and the anti-interventionist right define themselves explicitly against AIPAC and the pro-Israel Republican establishment.
- That fight runs directly through this network, because [[Palantir Technologies]] and [[Alex Karp]] sit firmly on the pro-Israel side via the [[Israel Ministry of Defense]] partnership, while [[JD Vance]]'s political base overlaps with Carlson's audience.

## Connections
- [[Tucker Carlson]], [[Nick Fuentes]], [[Steve Bannon]] — the wing that campaigns against it.
- [[Israel Ministry of Defense]], [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Alex Karp]] — the side of the argument this network's flagship company is on.
- [[JD Vance]] — caught between a pro-Israel administration and an anti-interventionist base.
