> [!abstract] Founders Fund
> Founded 2005 by [[Peter Thiel]], [[Luke Nosek]] and [[Ken Howery]]. The institution that
> converted the PayPal network into permanent capital.

## Key facts
- Founding thesis: back founders, don't replace them — a direct reaction to the boards that ousted [[Elon Musk]] and others.
- Its 2011 manifesto "What Happened to the Future?" ("we wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters") set the tone for the network's stagnation argument.
- Positions include [[SpaceX]], [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Meta Platforms]], [[Anduril Industries]], Stripe, Airbnb, [[Affirm]] and Ramp.
- The SpaceX bet, championed by [[Luke Nosek]], is among the best-performing venture investments ever made.
- Unusually willing to fund defence and national-security companies when most of Silicon Valley would not — an early-mover advantage that defined the current defence-tech wave.
