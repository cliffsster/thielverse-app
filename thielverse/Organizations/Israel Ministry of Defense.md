> [!abstract] Israel Ministry of Defense
> Signed a strategic partnership with [[Palantir Technologies]] in January 2024 to supply
> technology for what both parties described as war-related missions.

## Key facts
- The partnership was announced weeks into the Gaza war and made Palantir one of the few US technology companies to state its position on the conflict openly rather than quietly.
- [[Alex Karp]] has been unusually vocal in support of Israel, holding a board meeting in Tel Aviv in January 2024 and publicly criticising peers and universities for their responses to the war.
- The stance places Palantir directly opposite the anti-interventionist right represented by [[Tucker Carlson]] and [[Nick Fuentes]] — the deepest fault line running through the coalition this network joined.

## Connections
- [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Alex Karp]] — the partnership and its most vocal defender.
