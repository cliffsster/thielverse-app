> [!abstract] The Stanford Review
> Conservative-libertarian student newspaper founded in 1987 by [[Peter Thiel]] and Norman Book.
> The ideological source code for a remarkable share of this graph.

## Key facts
- Founded in reaction to Stanford's Western Culture curriculum fight; Thiel and [[David Sacks]] later expanded that argument into *The Diversity Myth* (1995).
- Alumni who appear elsewhere in this vault: [[Peter Thiel]], [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]], [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Blake Masters]].
- Functions as the network's earliest filter — it selected for ideological alignment years before any money was involved, which is why the PayPal and Palantir founding groups are so politically coherent.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — founder.
- [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]], [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Blake Masters]] — editors and writers.
- [[Stanford University]] — host institution.
- [[PayPal]], [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Founders Fund]] — downstream organisations staffed from it.
