> [!abstract] 404 Media
> Journalist-owned outlet whose reporting drove the 2025 reckoning over [[Flock Safety]]'s licence-plate network and federal access to it.

## Key facts
- Founded in 2023 by former Motherboard journalists as a reader-funded cooperative.
- Obtained and published audit-log data showing local police running Flock lookups on behalf of federal agencies including [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] — outside the oversight Flock advertises.
- That reporting triggered state investigations, contract cancellations and the ending of a CBP pilot.
- Demonstrates that a four-person outlet with document access can move this network's regulatory environment more than most institutional coverage.
