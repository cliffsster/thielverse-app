> [!abstract] Valar Ventures
> Fintech-focused fund founded 2010 by [[Peter Thiel]], [[Andrew McCormack]] and James
> Fitzgerald, built around the thesis that great fintech companies would be founded outside
> Silicon Valley.

## Key facts
- Named for the Valar of Tolkien's legendarium.
- Started with a New Zealand focus (Xero), then broadened to Europe and the US.
- Positions include N26, Wise (TransferWise), Stash and Bitpanda.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — co-founder.
- [[Andrew McCormack]] — PayPal alum and co-founder.
- [[Founders Fund]], [[Mithril Capital]], [[Clarium Capital]] — sibling Thiel vehicles.
