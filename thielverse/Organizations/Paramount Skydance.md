> [!abstract] Paramount Skydance
> Formed by Skydance's August 2025 merger with Paramount Global, run by [[David Ellison]] and financed by [[Larry Ellison]]. Owns CBS.

## Key facts
- Bought [[The Free Press]] two months after the merger closed and made [[Bari Weiss]] Editor-in-Chief of CBS News.
- Pursued Warner Bros. Discovery in the ensuing consolidation wave.
- The transaction required FCC sign-off under chairman [[Brendan Carr]], whose tenure has featured explicit pressure on broadcast news programming.
- Represents the arrival of tech-adjacent money in legacy broadcast at a moment when the regulator is unusually engaged with editorial content.

## Connections
- [[David Ellison]], [[Larry Ellison]] — control and financing.
- [[Bari Weiss]], [[The Free Press]] — the editorial acquisition.
- [[Brendan Carr]], [[Federal Communications Commission]] — the regulator.
- [[Rupert Murdoch]], [[Fox News]] — the incumbent it competes with.
