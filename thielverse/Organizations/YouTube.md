> [!abstract] YouTube
> Founded February 2005 by three [[PayPal]] employees — [[Chad Hurley]], [[Steve Chen]] and
> [[Jawed Karim]]. Google bought it for $1.65B in November 2006.

## Key facts
- Seed funding came from [[Roelof Botha]] at [[Sequoia Capital]] — one PayPal alum writing the first cheque to three others. The purest demonstration of how the network compounds.
- Went from founding to $1.65B exit in 21 months.
- Karim uploaded the first video, *Me at the zoo*, and stepped back early with a smaller stake.

## Connections
- [[Chad Hurley]], [[Steve Chen]], [[Jawed Karim]] — founders.
- [[Roelof Botha]], [[Sequoia Capital]] — seed investor.
- [[PayPal]] — common employer.
