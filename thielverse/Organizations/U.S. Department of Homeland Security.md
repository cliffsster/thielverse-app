> [!abstract] U.S. Department of Homeland Security
> Parent department of [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] and
> [[U.S. Customs and Border Protection]] — the two agencies through which this network's
> technology touches the most people directly.

## Key facts
- ICE runs [[Palantir Technologies]] case-management and investigative systems.
- CBP deploys [[Anduril Industries]] autonomous surveillance towers along the southern border, a programme that began in 2018 and expanded substantially.
- [[Flock Safety]]'s licence-plate network became a DHS-adjacent data source in 2025 through informal local-police lookups rather than direct contracts.
- DHS is where the three tiers of this network's surveillance stack — federal platform, border hardware, municipal camera network — converge on the same population.
