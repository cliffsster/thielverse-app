> [!abstract] Open Philanthropy
> Grantmaking organisation funded principally by Dustin Moskovitz and Cari Tuna; the main
> institutional home of effective altruism and an early funder of AI-safety work including
> [[Anthropic]]'s intellectual lineage.

## Key facts
- Directed large sums into AI safety research, biosecurity and global health from 2014 onward.
- The EA/AI-safety worldview it funds is the direct philosophical opponent of [[Marc Andreessen]]'s *Techno-Optimist Manifesto* and of the accelerationist position [[David Sacks]] argues from inside government.
- Its reputation was damaged by the FTX collapse, which also cost [[Anthropic]] a major shareholder.
- Included here as the counter-pole: the same era, the same money, the opposite conclusion about what technology requires.
