> [!abstract] In-Q-Tel
> The CIA's non-profit venture capital arm, founded 1999 with [[Gilman Louie]] as founding CEO.
> The institution that made [[Palantir Technologies]] real.

## Key facts
- Created to let the intelligence community buy commercial technology instead of commissioning bespoke systems from primes — a structural change that made the entire defence-tech wave possible.
- Invested roughly $2m in Palantir around 2005 and, more importantly, put CIA analysts in front of the product for three years of iteration.
- Also an early investor in Keyhole (which became Google Earth), FireEye and Recorded Future.
- Its budget is a rounding error against CIA spending; its influence comes from validation, not capital.

## Connections
- [[Gilman Louie]] — founding CEO.
- [[Central Intelligence Agency]] — sponsor.
- [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Alex Karp]], [[Peter Thiel]] — its most consequential investment.
- [[Founders Fund]] — the private-capital counterpart in defence tech.

## Also connected
