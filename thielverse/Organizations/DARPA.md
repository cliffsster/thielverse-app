> [!abstract] DARPA
> The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency — where [[John Poindexter]]'s Total
> Information Awareness programme ran, and the intellectual ancestor of
> [[Palantir Technologies]]' core premise.

## Key facts
- TIA (2002–2003) aimed to fuse commercial and government data to detect terrorist planning in advance. Congress defunded it after public backlash.
- Palantir was founded in the same window with the same technical premise and an explicit privacy-and-audit layer — its founders' stated answer to why TIA failed politically.
- DARPA's broader model of funding high-risk research that industry then commercialises is the same logic [[In-Q-Tel]] applied to venture capital.

- [[John Poindexter]] — ran the Information Awareness Office.
- [[Palantir Technologies]] — the commercial successor argument.
