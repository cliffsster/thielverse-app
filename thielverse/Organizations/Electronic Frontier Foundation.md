> [!abstract] Electronic Frontier Foundation
> Digital civil-liberties organisation; the primary technical documentarian of the
> surveillance systems this network builds.

## Key facts
- Runs the Atlas of Surveillance, which maps police technology deployments across the US including [[Flock Safety]] licence-plate readers and Palantir installations.
- Its Street-Level Surveillance work is the main public reference for how these systems function in practice, as distinct from how vendors describe them.
- Central to the 2025 reporting cycle on federal access to Flock's ALPR data.
- Opposes the argument [[Alex Karp]] and [[Garrett Langley]] both make — that audit logs and access controls make mass data collection acceptable — on the grounds that the collection itself is the harm.

## Connections
- [[Flock Safety]], [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Anduril Industries]] — documentation targets.
- [[National Security Agency]], [[U.S. Customs and Border Protection]].
- [[American Civil Liberties Union]], [[Mijente]] — allied opposition.
