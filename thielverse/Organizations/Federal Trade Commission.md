> [!abstract] Federal Trade Commission
> Under [[Lina Khan]] (2021–2025), the most aggressive antitrust enforcer this industry had faced in decades — and a primary motivation for tech's 2024 political realignment.

## Key facts
- Khan brought monopolisation cases against Amazon and [[Meta Platforms]], challenged acquisitions aggressively, and banned most non-compete clauses.
- [[Marc Andreessen]], [[David Sacks]] and [[Elon Musk]] campaigned against her publicly; opposition to the FTC's posture was an explicit theme of 2024 tech donations.
- Under the second Trump administration the agency narrowed the Khan agenda while continuing several big-tech cases — a partial rather than total reversal.
