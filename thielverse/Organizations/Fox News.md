> [!abstract] Fox News
> [[Rupert Murdoch]]'s cable network; [[Tucker Carlson]]'s employer from 2016 until his firing in April 2023.

## Key facts
- Settled the Dominion Voting Systems defamation suit for $787.5m in April 2023; Carlson was dismissed days later.
- His departure fragmented the conservative media audience into independent networks, podcasts and streaming — the ecosystem [[1789 Capital]] and [[Substack]] now serve.
- Still the largest single conservative media institution, and the benchmark the newer outlets measure themselves against.
