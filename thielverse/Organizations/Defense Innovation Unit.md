> [!abstract] Defense Innovation Unit
> Pentagon office created in 2015 to buy commercial technology directly, led 2016–2018 by
> [[Raj Shah]]. The procedural change that made this network's business model viable at scale.

## Key facts
- Uses Other Transaction Agreements to contract with commercial companies in months rather than years, bypassing traditional defence acquisition.
- Its existence is the practical answer to the complaint [[Shyam Sankar]] and [[Christian Brose]] make publicly — and its expansion was a core recommendation of the [[National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence]].
- [[Palantir Technologies]] and [[Anduril Industries]] are among the clearest beneficiaries of the contracting pathways DIU normalised.
