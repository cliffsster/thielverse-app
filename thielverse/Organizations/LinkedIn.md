> [!abstract] LinkedIn
> Founded 2003 by [[Reid Hoffman]] and colleagues; sold to Microsoft for $26.2B in 2016 —
> the largest single exit from the [[PayPal]] alumni network.

## Key facts
- Hoffman started it immediately after the eBay sale, funded partly by PayPal proceeds.
- [[Keith Rabois]] ran business development there before Square.
- [[Greylock Partners]] and Sequoia were early backers; Hoffman later became a Greylock partner himself.

## Connections
- [[Reid Hoffman]] — founder.
- [[Keith Rabois]] — early executive.
- [[PayPal]], [[Greylock Partners]].
