> [!abstract] Federal Communications Commission
> Chaired by [[Brendan Carr]] since 2025; approves the broadcast-licence transfers that media consolidation depends on, and under Carr has applied unusually direct pressure to news programming.

## Key facts
- Carr wrote the [[Heritage Foundation]] Project 2025 chapter on the agency before taking the chair.
- Opened proceedings against broadcast networks and made public comments about licences during the September 2025 suspension of a late-night ABC programme — widely read as regulatory leverage over editorial decisions.
- Sits in the path of [[Paramount Skydance]]'s transactions, including the CBS acquisition and subsequent consolidation attempts.
