> [!abstract] NHS England
> Awarded [[Palantir Technologies]] the Federated Data Platform contract in November 2023,
> worth roughly £330m over seven years — the company's largest civilian government deal and
> its most politically contested.

## Key facts
- The FDP integrates patient and operational data across NHS trusts for capacity planning, elective recovery and supply chain.
- Palantir's route in ran through pandemic-era COVID-19 datastore work awarded initially for £1, which critics characterised as a foothold strategy.
- Opposed by the British Medical Association, Doctors' Association UK and privacy groups; challenged in court and subject to sustained parliamentary scrutiny.
- [[Louis Mosley]] led the campaign to win it.
