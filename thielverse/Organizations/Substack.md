> [!abstract] Substack
> Subscription newsletter platform backed by [[Andreessen Horowitz]]; the distribution layer for much of the heterodox and tech-right commentariat.

## Key facts
- a16z led its Series B in 2021 and has remained a significant backer.
- Hosted [[The Free Press]] through its growth into an acquirable media property — the clearest proof of the platform's thesis.
- Its refusal to moderate on the terms legacy platforms adopted made it the default home for writers exiting mainstream outlets, and a recurring target of criticism for what that permitted.

## Connections
- [[Andreessen Horowitz]], [[Marc Andreessen]] — investor.
- [[Bari Weiss]], [[The Free Press]] — its most commercially successful publication.
- [[Curtis Yarvin]], [[Balaji Srinivasan]] — part of the writer base it serves.
