> [!abstract] X.com
> [[Elon Musk]]'s online bank, founded 1999 with proceeds from the Zip2 sale. Merged with
> [[Confinity]] in March 2000 to form what became [[PayPal]].

## Key facts
- Musk funded it with roughly $12m of his own money and recruited [[Bill Harris]] of Intuit as CEO.
- The merged company fought internally over technology stack — Musk wanted Microsoft, the Confinity engineers wanted Unix — a dispute that contributed to his September 2000 ouster in favour of [[Peter Thiel]].
- Musk bought the x.com domain back from PayPal in 2017 and reused the name for Twitter in 2023.

## Connections
- [[Confinity]], [[PayPal]] — merger and successor.
- [[Elon Musk]], [[Bill Harris]], [[Jeremy Stoppelman]] — founder, CEO and early engineer.
