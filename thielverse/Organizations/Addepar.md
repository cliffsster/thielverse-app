> [!abstract] Addepar
> Wealth-management data platform founded 2009 by [[Joe Lonsdale]] and Jason Mirra.

## Key facts
- Applies the Palantir data-integration pattern to private wealth: aggregating fragmented portfolio data into one analysable model.
- Tracks trillions of dollars in assets for family offices, RIAs and private banks.
- Lonsdale's first company after leaving day-to-day work at [[Palantir Technologies]] — the clearest evidence that the founders saw the product as a general data-integration idea, not a defence one.
