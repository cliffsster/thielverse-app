> [!abstract] OpenAI
> Founded 2015 by [[Sam Altman]], [[Elon Musk]] and others as a nonprofit AI lab.
> [[Peter Thiel]] was among the original announced backers.

## Key facts
- Musk left the board in 2018 and later sued over the shift to a capped-profit and then restructured corporate form; he and Altman are now direct competitors via xAI.
- [[Reid Hoffman]] was an investor and board member until 2023.
- Altman was fired and reinstated over five days in November 2023.
- The company's federal policy environment is now shaped by [[David Sacks]] and [[Michael Kratsios]] — both from this network.
