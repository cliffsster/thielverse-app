> [!abstract] Affirm
> Buy-now-pay-later lender founded 2012 by [[Max Levchin]], [[Nathan Gettings]], Alex Rampell
> and Jeff Kaditz out of Levchin's HVF incubator.

## Key facts
- The single company that links [[PayPal]], [[Palantir Technologies]] and modern fintech through one person — Gettings, who was a PayPal fraud engineer, Palantir's first CTO, and then an Affirm co-founder.
- Underwriting relies on the same kind of behavioural risk modelling that PayPal built to survive fraud.
- IPO'd January 2021; [[Founders Fund]] and [[Brian Singerman]] were investors.

## Connections
- [[Max Levchin]] — co-founder and CEO.
- [[Nathan Gettings]] — co-founder; the PayPal→Palantir→Affirm bridge.
- [[Founders Fund]], [[Brian Singerman]], [[Scott Banister]] — investors.
