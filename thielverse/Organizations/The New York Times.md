> [!abstract] The New York Times
> The outlet that has done the most sustained investigative reporting on [[Palantir Technologies]]' government work — and is separately suing [[OpenAI]].

## Key facts
- Broke or advanced much of the public record on Palantir's [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] contracts, predictive-policing deployments and federal data consolidation.
- Sued OpenAI and Microsoft in December 2023 over the use of its archive in training data — the most significant copyright case against a frontier lab.
- [[Bari Weiss]]'s 2020 resignation letter from its opinion desk launched the heterodox-media wave that now runs CBS News.
- Functions in this graph as an adversarial node: most of what the vault documents about this network became public through reporting like this.
