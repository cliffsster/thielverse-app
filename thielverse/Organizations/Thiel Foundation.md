> [!abstract] Thiel Foundation
> [[Peter Thiel]]'s philanthropic vehicle, funding anti-ageing research, seasteading,
> AI-safety work (including early MIRI grants) and the [[Thiel Fellowship]].

## Key facts
- Ran by [[Blake Masters]] as president for several years; [[Jack Selby]] has also been closely involved.
- Funded SENS Research Foundation on longevity, led by [[Jim O'Neill]].
- Backed the Seasteading Institute with Patri Friedman — an early expression of the exit-from-democracy strand that recurs in the network's politics.
- Its grantmaking is best understood as ideological rather than charitable: each programme argues that an existing institution should be bypassed rather than reformed.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — founder and funder.
- [[Blake Masters]] — former president.
- [[Jim O'Neill]] — SENS Research Foundation CEO.
- [[Thiel Fellowship]] — its best-known programme.
- [[Curtis Yarvin]] — adjacent in the "exit over voice" intellectual tradition it funds.
