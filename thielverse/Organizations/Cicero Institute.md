> [!abstract] Cicero Institute
> State-policy think tank founded 2016 by [[Joe Lonsdale]] in Austin.

## Key facts
- Drafts model legislation for state legislatures on homelessness, healthcare pricing, occupational licensing and public-sector transparency.
- Its homelessness model bills — encampment bans paired with mandated treatment — have been adopted in multiple states and are heavily contested by housing researchers.
- Completes Lonsdale's stack: [[8VC]] supplies capital, [[OpenGov]] supplies the software, Cicero supplies the policy that creates demand for both.
