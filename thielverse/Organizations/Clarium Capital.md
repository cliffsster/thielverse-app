> [!abstract] Clarium Capital
> [[Peter Thiel]]'s global macro hedge fund, founded 2002 with roughly $10m of his PayPal
> proceeds. Peaked above $7B in assets around 2008, then lost most of it.

## Key facts
- Made large directional bets on energy prices, the dollar and the housing collapse; called 2008 correctly and then gave the gains back in 2009–2010.
- Assets fell to a few hundred million by 2011; it effectively became a family office.
- Functioned as a training ground: [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Jack Selby]], [[Jim O'Neill]] and [[Ajay Royan]] all passed through.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — founder.
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — early analyst there before co-founding [[Palantir Technologies]].
- [[Jack Selby]], [[Jim O'Neill]], [[Ajay Royan]] — managing directors and staff.
- [[Founders Fund]], [[Mithril Capital]] — sibling vehicles.
