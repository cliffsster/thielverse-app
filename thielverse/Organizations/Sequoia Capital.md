> [!abstract] Sequoia Capital
> Venture firm led by [[PayPal]] alum [[Roelof Botha]] as senior steward from 2022.

## Key facts
- Botha joined in 2003 straight from PayPal's CFO seat and led its [[YouTube]], Instagram and Square investments.
- Was an investor in PayPal itself, and Michael Moritz sat on its board — a pre-existing tie that made Botha's move natural.
- The firm's leadership by a PayPal alum is a quiet measure of how completely the network absorbed Silicon Valley's institutions.
