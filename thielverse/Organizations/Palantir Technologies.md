> [!abstract] Palantir Technologies
> Founded 2003 by [[Peter Thiel]], [[Alex Karp]], [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Stephen Cohen]] and
> [[Nathan Gettings]]. Named for the *palantír* seeing-stones in Tolkien.

## Key facts
- Premise: apply [[PayPal]]'s anti-fraud pattern-detection to counterterrorism intelligence, with access controls and audit logs built in.
- [[In-Q-Tel]], the CIA's venture arm, invested ~$2m in 2005; the [[Central Intelligence Agency]] became the first customer and CIA analysts effectively co-developed the early product.
- Products: **Gotham** (defence/intelligence), **Foundry** (commercial), **Apollo** (deployment), **AIP** (LLM integration, launched 2023).
- Pioneered the "forward-deployed engineer" model — its own staff embedded inside customer agencies. [[Shyam Sankar]] built it.
- Direct listing on the NYSE (PLTR) on 30 September 2020, with a share structure preserving founder control.
- Customers include the [[U.S. Department of Defense]], [[Central Intelligence Agency]], FBI, [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], the CDC, NHS England and a growing commercial base.
- Persistently controversial: ICE deportation operations, predictive policing pilots, the £330m NHS Federated Data Platform contract, and its work in Israel and Ukraine.
