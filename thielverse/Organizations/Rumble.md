> [!abstract] Rumble
> Video platform positioned as a free-speech alternative to YouTube; an investment of [[JD Vance]]'s [[Narya Capital]].

## Key facts
- Went public via SPAC in 2022; Narya was an early backer, giving the sitting Vice President a documented prior financial interest in a partisan media platform.
- Also backed by [[Peter Thiel]] in the funding rounds around that period.
- Hosts much of the independent right-wing video ecosystem, including content from figures whose distribution options elsewhere are limited.

## Connections
- [[JD Vance]], [[Narya Capital]] — investor.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — backer.
- [[Tucker Carlson]], [[1789 Capital]] — the adjacent independent-media economy.
- [[YouTube]] — the incumbent it defines itself against.
