> [!abstract] Mijente
> Latinx organising network that ran the **#NoTechForICE** campaign — the most effective
> direct pressure campaign against [[Palantir Technologies]] to date.

## Key facts
- Launched #NoTechForICE in 2018, targeting Palantir's [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] contracts through campus recruiting disruption, shareholder pressure and public reporting.
- Drove student protests that led several universities to restrict Palantir recruiting, and pushed the contracts into mainstream press coverage.
- Its research arm published the most detailed public accounting of how Palantir systems are used in immigration enforcement operations.
- [[Alex Karp]] has responded to this campaign directly and repeatedly — one of the few external pressures that has visibly shaped his public positioning.
