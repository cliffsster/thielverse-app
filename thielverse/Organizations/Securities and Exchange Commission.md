> [!abstract] Securities and Exchange Commission
> Regulator of [[Palantir Technologies]]' 2020 direct listing, and — under the second Trump administration — of the crypto regime [[David Sacks]] helps set.

## Key facts
- Palantir's September 2020 direct listing used a triple-class share structure preserving founder control; the SEC reviewed but did not block it.
- Reversed much of the prior enforcement-first approach to digital assets in 2025, dropping or settling major cases as the administration's crypto agenda advanced.
- [[Chamath Palihapitiya]]'s SPAC vehicles drew SEC scrutiny during the 2020–21 boom.
