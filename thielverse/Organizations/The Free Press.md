> [!abstract] The Free Press
> Founded by [[Bari Weiss]] in 2021 as a [[Substack]] newsletter; acquired by [[Paramount Skydance]] in October 2025 for a reported ~$150m, with Weiss installed as Editor-in-Chief of CBS News.

## Key facts
- Built on the heterodox-media thesis: that mainstream outlets had abandoned viewpoint diversity and a readership existed for the gap.
- Shares founders and worldview with the [[University of Austin]] — [[Joe Lonsdale]] is a co-founder of the university, Weiss of both.
- The acquisition is the moment this network's media ecosystem stopped being an alternative to legacy media and started running part of it.
