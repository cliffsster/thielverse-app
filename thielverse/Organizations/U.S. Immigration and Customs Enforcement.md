> [!abstract] U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
> [[Palantir Technologies]]' most contested customer relationship.

## Key facts
- Palantir has supplied ICE with case-management and investigative software since the early 2010s, including the FALCON and later ImmigrationOS systems.
- The contracts triggered sustained employee protests at Palantir and at partner companies, campus recruiting bans, and public campaigns from immigrant-rights groups from 2018 onward.
- [[Alex Karp]] has defended the work directly and repeatedly, distinguishing between enforcement of law and policy he personally opposes — one of the few places he has taken public heat without hedging.
- Reporting through 2025 described substantially expanded ICE contracts under the second Trump administration.
