> [!abstract] Craft Ventures
> Early-stage fund founded 2017 by [[David Sacks]] and Bill Lee, focused on SaaS and
> marketplaces.

## Key facts
- Sacks' vehicle after Yammer and Zenefits; portfolio includes SpaceX, Slack, Reddit, Bird and ClickUp.
- Became a political as much as a financial platform, with Sacks hosting fundraisers and using the *All-In* podcast to shape tech's rightward shift in 2023–24.
- Sacks stepped into a White House role in 2025 while retaining ties to the firm — a conflict-of-interest question raised repeatedly in reporting.

## Connections
- [[David Sacks]] — co-founder.
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Elon Musk]] — long-running co-investors and allies.
- [[Donald Trump]] — the administration Sacks joined.
