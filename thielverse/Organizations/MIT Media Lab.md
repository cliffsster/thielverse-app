> [!abstract] MIT Media Lab
> The institution through which [[Jeffrey Epstein]] bought post-conviction proximity to elite science — and the point where the Epstein story touches [[Reid Hoffman]].

## Key facts
- Director [[Joi Ito]] accepted Epstein donations after the 2008 conviction and recorded them as anonymous to conceal the source; Ito resigned in September 2019 after *The New Yorker* reported it.
- Epstein was also used as an intermediary to solicit gifts from wealthier donors, which is how he converted money into access to researchers.
- Hoffman was a Media Lab funder in this period and apologised publicly for his part in restoring Epstein's standing.

## Connections
- [[Jeffrey Epstein]], [[Joi Ito]] — the donations and the concealment.
- [[Reid Hoffman]] — funder; the bridge into this vault.
- [[Larry Summers]] — the other academic-world figure whose Epstein contact later became public.
