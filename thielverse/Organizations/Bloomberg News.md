> [!abstract] Bloomberg News
> Published the 2011 profile that first pulled [[Palantir Technologies]] into public view, at a point when the company had deliberately avoided press attention.

## Key facts
- The Businessweek profile described Palantir's intelligence-community work, its early [[In-Q-Tel]] backing and its founder mythology — much of what is now common knowledge about the company's origins traces to it.
- Has continued covering the company's contracts, valuation and political entanglements through its public-market phase.
- Its financial-data business also makes it a commercial peer to Palantir's enterprise-analytics ambitions.
