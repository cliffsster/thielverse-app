> [!abstract] Anduril Industries
> Founded 2017 by [[Palmer Luckey]], [[Trae Stephens]], [[Brian Schimpf]], [[Matt Grimm]] and
> Joe Chen. Named for Aragorn's sword — the Tolkien naming convention again.

## Key facts
- Explicitly Palantir-for-hardware: three of its five founders came from [[Palantir Technologies]], and it copied the model of building product first and selling it to the government rather than bidding on cost-plus requirements.
- Products: **Lattice** (autonomy software), Sentry surveillance towers on the US southern border, Anvil and Roadrunner counter-drone systems, Ghost and Bolt drones, Dive-LD underwater vehicles, and Fury autonomous fighter aircraft for the Air Force CCA programme.
- Took over Microsoft's troubled $22B IVAS Army headset programme in 2025.
- Backed from the start by [[Founders Fund]]; valued in the tens of billions by 2025.
- [[Christian Brose]], former staff director of the Senate Armed Services Committee, runs strategy — the Washington translation layer.
