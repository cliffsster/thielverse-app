> [!abstract] Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
> The regulator of buy-now-pay-later lending — [[Affirm]]'s market — effectively shut down in 2025 by [[Russ Vought]] and the [[Department of Government Efficiency]].

## Key facts
- Created after the 2008 crisis; issued the 2024 interpretive rule bringing BNPL products under credit-card-style protections.
- In early 2025 DOGE staff obtained access to its systems, its headquarters was closed, most work was ordered stopped and funding was withdrawn under Vought as acting director.
- The result: [[Max Levchin]]'s Affirm and its competitors operate in a market whose primary federal supervisor has been defunded.
