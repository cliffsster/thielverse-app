> [!abstract] University of Austin (UATX)
> Private university announced 2021, co-founded by a group including [[Joe Lonsdale]],
> Bari Weiss, Niall Ferguson and Pano Kanelos, framed as a response to perceived ideological
> conformity in higher education.

## Key facts
- Lonsdale is a founding trustee and major funder.
- Enrolled its first undergraduate class in 2024 and received Texas authorisation to grant degrees.
- Sits alongside the [[Thiel Fellowship]] as the network's second institutional attack on the university system — one bypasses it, the other tries to replace it.

- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — co-founder and trustee.
