> [!abstract] National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence (NSCAI)
> Congressionally mandated commission, 2018–2021, chaired by [[Eric Schmidt]] with
> [[Gilman Louie]] among its commissioners.

## Key facts
- Its 2021 final report argued the US was not prepared to compete with China on AI and recommended sweeping changes to defence procurement, talent pipelines and research funding.
- Much of the current defence-tech policy environment — and the budget lines [[Palantir Technologies]] and [[Anduril Industries]] now sell into — traces to its recommendations.
- Criticised for conflicts of interest: commissioners held positions in companies that stood to benefit from its recommendations.

## Connections
- [[Eric Schmidt]] — chairman.
- [[Gilman Louie]] — commissioner and [[In-Q-Tel]] founder.
- [[U.S. Department of Defense]], [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Anduril Industries]].
- [[Michael Kratsios]] — federal AI policy counterpart as US CTO in the same period.
