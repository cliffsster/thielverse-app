> [!abstract] Anthropic
> AI safety company founded 2021 by [[Dario Amodei]], [[Daniela Amodei]] and five other
> [[OpenAI]] researchers. Structurally the ideological opposite of this network — and
> commercially one of [[Palantir Technologies]]' partners.

## Key facts
- Public Benefit Corporation governed in part by a Long-Term Benefit Trust, designed to resist the commercial pressure that broke [[OpenAI]]'s nonprofit board in 2023.
- Builds Claude. Major investors include Google and Amazon; FTX/Alameda held roughly an 8% stake that was sold during bankruptcy proceedings.
- **November 2024**: partnered with [[Palantir Technologies]] and AWS to deploy Claude in US defence and intelligence environments up to Impact Level 6 — the direct commercial edge between Anthropic and this network. Criticised by AI-safety advocates as inconsistent with its positioning; defended by Anthropic as ensuring democracies lead.
- Released Claude Gov models for national-security customers in 2025.
- [[David Sacks]] publicly attacked Anthropic in 2025, accusing it of running a regulatory-capture strategy through fear — the sharpest open conflict between the administration's tech wing and a frontier lab.
- Funded in significant part, historically, by donors in the effective-altruism orbit ([[Open Philanthropy]]), the philosophical counterweight to [[Marc Andreessen]]'s techno-optimism.
