> [!abstract] OpenGov
> Government finance and operations software, founded 2012 with [[Joe Lonsdale]] as a
> co-founder.

## Key facts
- Sells budgeting, procurement and permitting software to US cities, counties and states — the local-government layer beneath Palantir's federal focus.
- Cox Enterprises acquired a majority stake in 2024 at a reported ~$1.8B valuation.
- Completes the Lonsdale pattern: Palantir for federal agencies, OpenGov for municipalities, [[Cicero Institute]] for the policy those municipalities adopt.

## Connections
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — co-founder.
- [[8VC]], [[Cicero Institute]] — the rest of his government-facing cluster.
- [[Palantir Technologies]] — same founder lineage, different tier of government.
