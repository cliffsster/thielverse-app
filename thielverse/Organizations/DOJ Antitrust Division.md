> [!abstract] DOJ Antitrust Division
> Led since 2025 by [[Gail Slater]], previously economic policy adviser to Senator [[JD Vance]] — the most direct line from the Vice President into an enforcement agency.

## Key facts
- Continued the Google search and advertising-technology remedies after the liability findings.
- Slater's confirmation, with Vance's public backing, kept a populist-antitrust programme alive inside an administration otherwise staffed from the industry being regulated.
- The division and the [[Federal Trade Commission]] split big-tech enforcement between them.
