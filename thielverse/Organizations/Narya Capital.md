> [!abstract] Narya Capital
> Cincinnati-based venture fund founded 2020 by [[JD Vance]], backed by [[Peter Thiel]],
> Marc Andreessen and [[Eric Schmidt]]. Named for one of Tolkien's Rings of Power.

## Key facts
- Vance's vehicle between leaving [[Mithril Capital]] and running for Senate; explicitly framed around investing outside the coasts.
- Investments included Rumble, AppHarvest and Hallow.
- The Thiel–Andreessen–Schmidt backing is one of the few places in this graph where the network's political factions co-invested.
- Vance divested and stepped back on entering the Senate in 2023.

## Connections
- [[JD Vance]] — founder.
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Eric Schmidt]] — backers.
- [[Mithril Capital]] — his previous employer and the naming lineage.

## Also connected
