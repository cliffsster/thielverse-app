> [!abstract] Heritage Foundation
> Conservative think tank led by [[Kevin Roberts]]; author of Project 2025, the personnel and
> policy blueprint whose recommendations track closely with the second Trump administration.

## Key facts
- Project 2025's central proposal — reclassifying and replacing large parts of the career civil service — is the institutional version of the argument [[Curtis Yarvin]] has made for years and that the [[Department of Government Efficiency]] executed.
- [[JD Vance]] wrote the foreword to a book by Roberts, tying him publicly to the organisation.
- Suffered its worst institutional crisis in 2025 when Roberts defended [[Tucker Carlson]] over the [[Nick Fuentes]] interview, producing donor losses and staff resignations.
- Heritage is where the intellectual right and this network's political project meet — a more conventional and more fragile institution than either.
