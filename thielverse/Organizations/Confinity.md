> [!abstract] Confinity
> Founded 1998 by [[Peter Thiel]], [[Max Levchin]], [[Luke Nosek]] and [[Ken Howery]].
> Originally cryptography for handheld devices; pivoted to Palm Pilot money transfer, then
> email payments — the product that became [[PayPal]].

## Key facts
- Thiel as CEO, Levchin as CTO, Howery as CFO, Nosek on marketing — the original four-way split.
- Its Palm-beaming demo gave way to the far more popular email-based payment feature, which spread virally on eBay.
- Merged with [[Elon Musk]]'s [[X.com]] in March 2000 after a costly promotional war between the two.
