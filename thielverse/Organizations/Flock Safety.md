> [!abstract] Flock Safety
> Founded 2017 by [[Garrett Langley]]. Operates the largest private automated
> licence-plate-reader network in the United States — the consumer-facing tier of the
> surveillance stack [[Palantir Technologies]] occupies at the federal level.

## Key facts
- Cameras deployed across several thousand US communities under contracts with police departments, HOAs and businesses; the network logs vehicle movements and retains the data for search.
- Backed by [[Andreessen Horowitz]] and by [[Bedrock Capital]], the fund [[Geoff Lewis]] started after leaving [[Founders Fund]] — the capital link into this network.
- Its Nova product aggregates third-party data sources on top of plate reads, extending it beyond vehicle tracking.
- **2025 scrutiny**: reporting showed local police running Flock lookups on behalf of federal agencies including [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]], often outside the audit trails Flock advertises. Several states opened investigations, some agencies cancelled contracts, and a CBP pilot was ended after backlash.
- Langley's public defence — audit logs, narrow purpose, crime reduction — is the same argument [[Alex Karp]] makes for Palantir, at a tier with far weaker oversight.
