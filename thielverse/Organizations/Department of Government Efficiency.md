> [!abstract] Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE)
> Created by executive order in January 2025 and led initially by [[Elon Musk]] as a special
> government employee.

## Key facts
- Restructured the US Digital Service into a body tasked with cutting federal headcount, contracts and regulations.
- Drove mass reductions across federal agencies and gained access to Treasury, OPM and agency payment systems — prompting multiple lawsuits over privacy and statutory authority.
- Musk departed in May 2025 as his 130-day special-employee window closed, then publicly broke with [[Donald Trump]].
- Its animating idea — that the permanent civil service should be dismantled rather than reformed — closely tracks arguments [[Curtis Yarvin]] has made for over a decade.

## Connections
- [[Elon Musk]] — initial leader.
- [[Donald Trump]] — created it by executive order.
- [[Curtis Yarvin]] — the clearest intellectual antecedent, with no formal role.
- [[Palantir Technologies]] — reported to have supplied data-integration work to agencies during the same period.
