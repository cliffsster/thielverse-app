> [!abstract] Remilia Corporation
> The collective behind Milady Maker, founded by [[Charlotte Fang]] in 2021. Present in this
> vault as **cultural weather**, not as a network member.

## Key facts
- Milady Maker is a 10,000-piece pixel-art NFT collection built around a deliberately unsettling internet-native aesthetic and a stated "network spirituality" ideology.
- [[Elon Musk]] posted a Milady image in May 2024, causing a sharp spike in floor price — the single concrete contact point with anyone in this vault.
- The aesthetic became a common visual signature for a strand of very-online right-adjacent culture, sharing an audience with the [[Curtis Yarvin]] milieu without any organisational or financial link.
- Fang stepped back in 2022 after being identified with a prior pseudonymous account tied to a harmful online community, then resumed involvement.
