> [!abstract] Yelp
> Founded 2004 by [[PayPal]] engineers [[Jeremy Stoppelman]] and [[Russel Simmons]], incubated
> and seed-funded by [[Max Levchin]].

## Key facts
- Levchin's MRL Ventures provided the first $1m and office space — the network's standard pattern of a senior alum backing juniors.
- IPO'd in 2012; Stoppelman has remained CEO throughout.
- Levchin remained chairman for years.

## Connections
- [[Jeremy Stoppelman]], [[Russel Simmons]] — founders.
- [[Max Levchin]] — incubator and funder.
- [[PayPal]].
