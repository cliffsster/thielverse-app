> [!abstract] Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)
> Founded 2009 by [[Marc Andreessen]] and Ben Horowitz. The main capital rival — and frequent
> co-investor — to [[Founders Fund]] in defence and government technology.

## Key facts
- Its American Dynamism practice, led by [[Katherine Boyle]], invests in defence, aerospace, manufacturing and public-safety companies: [[Anduril Industries]], [[Flock Safety]], Shield AI, Saronic, Hadrian.
- Pushed the "Little Tech" agenda in Washington against AI regulation, aligned with [[David Sacks]] and [[Y Combinator]] and against [[Anthropic]]'s position.
- Andreessen and Horowitz both endorsed [[Donald Trump]] in 2024 and advised on transition personnel.
- Structurally different from Founders Fund: bigger, more institutional, less ideologically Tolkien.
