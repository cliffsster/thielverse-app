> [!abstract] Mithril Capital
> Growth-stage fund founded 2012 by [[Peter Thiel]] and [[Ajay Royan]]. Best known outside
> finance as [[JD Vance]]'s employer.

## Key facts
- Named after the Tolkien metal, continuing the naming convention that also produced Palantir and Valar.
- Focuses on later-stage technology, with concentrations in healthcare, life sciences and security.
- Employed [[JD Vance]] as a principal in 2016–17 — the job that professionalised his relationship with Thiel.
- [[Jim O'Neill]], now Deputy Secretary of HHS, was a managing director.
- Relocated from San Francisco to Austin.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Ajay Royan]] — co-founders.
- [[JD Vance]] — former principal.
- [[Jim O'Neill]] — former MD.
- [[Narya Capital]] — Vance's own fund, seeded by Thiel afterwards.
