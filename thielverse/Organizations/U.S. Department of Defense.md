> [!abstract] U.S. Department of Defense
> The customer that both [[Palantir Technologies]] and [[Anduril Industries]] were built to
> displace incumbents inside.

## Key facts
- Palantir sued the Army in 2016 over the DCGS-A programme — arguing the Army was legally required to consider commercial products — and won on appeal in 2018. That ruling changed federal software procurement.
- Palantir now holds Army programmes including TITAN and the Maven Smart System.
- Anduril took over the $22B IVAS soldier-headset programme from Microsoft in 2025 and builds for the Air Force's Collaborative Combat Aircraft programme.
- Network figures with direct DoD roles: [[Trae Stephens]] (2016 transition team), [[Michael Kratsios]] (acting Under Secretary for R&E), [[Christian Brose]] (Senate Armed Services staff director), [[Eric Schmidt]] (Defense Innovation Board chair).
