> [!abstract] Internal Revenue Service
> Subject of 2025 reporting that [[Palantir Technologies]] was building a consolidated API
> over IRS taxpayer data — one of the most consequential and least examined expansions of
> the company's federal footprint.

## Key facts
- Reporting in 2025 described Palantir engineers working alongside [[Department of Government Efficiency]] staff on a "mega-API" to make IRS records queryable across systems.
- The concern raised by privacy advocates and by IRS staff was not the technology but the removal of the compartmentalisation that has historically limited what any single federal system can see about a person.
- Sits alongside similar 2025 reporting about data consolidation at the Social Security Administration and HHS.
