> [!abstract] Federal Bureau of Investigation
> Long-standing [[Palantir Technologies]] customer for case management and investigative
> data integration.

## Key facts
- Palantir has held FBI contracts for over a decade, including a large data-management system replacing legacy case tools.
- FBI usage is the domestic-law-enforcement end of the same platform sold to the [[Central Intelligence Agency]] — the transition that draws most civil-liberties objection, since domestic data carries constitutional protections foreign intelligence does not.
