> [!abstract] All-In Podcast
> Hosted by [[David Sacks]], [[Chamath Palihapitiya]], Jason Calacanis and David Friedberg; the most politically consequential podcast in technology.

## Key facts
- Launched in 2020 as a pandemic-era conversation among four investor friends; became a primary channel for tech's rightward shift in 2023–24.
- Hosted candidate interviews and fundraisers during the 2024 cycle; two of its four hosts became significant Trump backers, and one became a White House official.
- Sacks continued as a host after taking the AI & Crypto Czar role, an arrangement that drew persistent conflict-of-interest criticism.
- The clearest example of this network owning its own distribution rather than seeking coverage.
