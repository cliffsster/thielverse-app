> [!abstract] Jeremy Stoppelman
> [[PayPal]] engineer turned co-founder and CEO of [[Yelp]].

## Key facts
- Joined [[X.com]] as an engineer in 2000 and stayed through the PayPal merger and eBay sale; became VP of engineering.
- Co-founded [[Yelp]] in 2004 with [[Russel Simmons]], incubated and funded by [[Max Levchin]]'s MRL Ventures.
- Took Yelp public in 2012 and has remained CEO throughout.
- One of the more publicly progressive PayPal alumni; a persistent critic of Google's search practices and of Trump.

## Connections
- [[Max Levchin]] — funder and incubator of Yelp; his most important professional relationship.
- [[Russel Simmons]] — PayPal colleague and Yelp co-founder.
- [[Elon Musk]] — hired him at X.com.
- [[Yelp]], [[PayPal]] — his two companies.
