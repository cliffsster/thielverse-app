> [!danger] Read this before using this cluster
> Epstein cultivated relationships with scientists, academics, financiers and technologists — many of whom had no knowledge of or involvement in his crimes. **Appearing on this page means a contact is documented in public record. It is not an allegation of wrongdoing.** Each edge below states what the documented contact actually was. Do not collapse "attended a dinner" into anything more than that.

## Key facts
- American financier, born 1953. Pleaded guilty in 2008 to Florida state charges of soliciting prostitution from a minor, serving 13 months under a widely criticised non-prosecution agreement.
- Arrested July 2019 on federal sex-trafficking charges; died in custody in August 2019, ruled a suicide.
- Spent the decade **after** his 2008 conviction rebuilding access to elite scientific and technology circles — funding academic institutions and convening dinners. That post-conviction period is what makes the contacts below notable, since his record was public by then.
- The Epstein Files Transparency Act was signed in November 2025, compelling Justice Department releases; House Oversight separately published estate documents and correspondence.

## Documented contacts in this vault
- [[Reid Hoffman]] — **the strongest edge here.** Met Epstein several times after the conviction and helped connect him to the [[MIT Media Lab]] fundraising circle. Hoffman apologised publicly in 2019, saying his participation had helped rehabilitate Epstein's reputation.
- [[Larry Summers]] — sustained correspondence continuing to 2019; the November 2025 release of those emails ended his public roles, including his [[OpenAI]] board seat.
- [[Joi Ito]] — MIT Media Lab director who took Epstein's money and concealed it; resigned in 2019.
- [[Elon Musk]] — photographed beside [[Ghislaine Maxwell]] at a 2014 party (Musk said she photobombed the shot); says he visited Epstein's Manhattan house once, briefly, and had no relationship with him.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — has acknowledged meeting Epstein on a small number of occasions, including dinners; publicly speculated about Epstein's possible intelligence links in his 2025 [[Tucker Carlson]] interview.
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]] — named in reporting on Epstein's post-conviction outreach to technology figures; Meta has said Zuckerberg had no relationship with him.
- [[Steve Bannon]] — conducted many hours of filmed interviews with Epstein and advised him on media strategy after the conviction.
- [[Donald Trump]] — socialised with Epstein extensively in the 1990s and early 2000s; the handling of the files became a major fight inside his own coalition in 2025.
- [[Pam Bondi]] — as Attorney General, ran the 2025 releases whose handling triggered that fight.
