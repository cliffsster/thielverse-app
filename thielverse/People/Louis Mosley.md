> [!abstract] Louis Mosley
> Executive Vice President for the UK and Europe at [[Palantir Technologies]]; the face of the
> company's controversial NHS work.

## Key facts
- Joined Palantir in 2016 after a career in finance and a run as a Conservative council candidate.
- Led Palantir's expansion in Britain, culminating in the 2023 NHS England Federated Data Platform contract worth roughly £330m — the most contested public-sector deal in the company's history.
- Grandson of Oswald Mosley, the 1930s British fascist leader — a fact British media raised repeatedly during the NHS contract debate, and which Mosley has addressed publicly.
