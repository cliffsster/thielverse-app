> [!abstract] Ken Howery
> Co-founder and first CFO of [[Confinity]]/[[PayPal]], co-founder of [[Founders Fund]], and
> a US ambassador under both Trump administrations. The clearest personal example of the
> network moving into formal state office.

## Key facts
- Born 1975 in Texas. Stanford BA economics; the only Confinity co-founder recruited straight out of undergrad, by [[Peter Thiel]].
- PayPal's first CFO through the eBay acquisition.
- Co-founded [[Founders Fund]] in 2005 with Thiel and [[Luke Nosek]]; led investments across space, defence and fintech.
- US Ambassador to Sweden, 2019–2021.
- Named by [[Donald Trump]] as US Ambassador to Denmark in December 2024 — a posting made unusually pointed by the administration's stated interest in Greenland.
- Relocated to Texas and became an active backer of the Austin tech scene alongside [[Joe Lonsdale]].

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Luke Nosek]], [[Max Levchin]] — Confinity co-founders.
- [[Founders Fund]] — co-founder.
- [[Donald Trump]] — twice-appointing authority.
- [[David Sacks]], [[Michael Kratsios]], [[Jim O'Neill]], [[JD Vance]] — fellow network members holding federal office.
