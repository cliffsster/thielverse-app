> [!abstract] Ajay Royan
> Co-founder and managing general partner of [[Mithril Capital]] with [[Peter Thiel]] — and
> [[JD Vance]]'s boss before Vance entered politics.

## Key facts
- Yale BA; worked at Clarium Capital before co-founding [[Mithril Capital]] with Thiel in 2012, named after the Tolkien metal, continuing Thiel's naming habit.
- Mithril focuses on later-stage technology companies, particularly in healthcare and defence-adjacent sectors.
- Employed [[JD Vance]] as a principal from 2016–17, the job that brought Vance into the Thiel orbit professionally.
- Moved Mithril's base from San Francisco to Austin.
