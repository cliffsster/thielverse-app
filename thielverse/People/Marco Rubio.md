> [!abstract] Marco Rubio
> Secretary of State since 2025, and concurrently National Security Adviser from May 2025 — an unusual dual role. [[Jacob Helberg]] serves under him.

## Key facts
- Florida senator 2011–2025; 2016 presidential candidate and a longtime foreign-policy hawk.
- Took both the State Department and the National Security Council portfolio in 2025, concentrating foreign-policy authority.
- Represents the interventionist, pro-Israel wing of the administration — the pole opposite [[Tucker Carlson]] and [[Nick Fuentes]] on the fault line running through this coalition.
- His department houses the economic-statecraft portfolio a serving Palantir adviser now runs.

- [[Israel Ministry of Defense]], [[AIPAC]] — the foreign-policy alignment.
- [[JD Vance]], [[Tucker Carlson]] — the restraint wing he is in tension with.
