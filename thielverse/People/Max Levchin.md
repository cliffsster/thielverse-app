> [!abstract] Max Levchin
> Co-founder and CTO of [[Confinity]]/[[PayPal]]; later founder of Slide and co-founder and
> CEO of [[Affirm]]. The technical half of the original Thiel partnership.

## Key facts
- Born 1975 in Kyiv, emigrated to Chicago in 1991. University of Illinois CS.
- Co-founded [[Confinity]] with [[Peter Thiel]] in 1998; built PayPal's fraud-detection systems — including the Gausebeck–Levchin test, one of the first commercial CAPTCHAs, with [[David Gausebeck]].
- PayPal's anti-fraud architecture is the direct intellectual ancestor of [[Palantir Technologies]]' original product.
- Founded Slide (2005, sold to Google 2010), then HVF, then [[Affirm]] in 2012 with [[Nathan Gettings]], Alex Rampell and Jeff Kaditz. Affirm IPO'd in 2021.
- Chaired Yahoo's board (2012–2015) and funded [[Jeremy Stoppelman]] and [[Russel Simmons]]' [[Yelp]] at its founding.
- Politically far less aligned with the Thiel right than most of this graph.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — co-founder; CEO/CTO pairing at Confinity and PayPal.
- [[Nathan Gettings]] — PayPal engineer and later Affirm co-founder; the strongest PayPal→Palantir→Affirm edge.
- [[Elon Musk]] — merger counterpart at [[X.com]]; the two clashed over technology stack.
- [[Jeremy Stoppelman]], [[Russel Simmons]] — funded their Yelp founding.
- [[David Gausebeck]] — co-author of the Gausebeck–Levchin test.
- [[Luke Nosek]], [[Ken Howery]] — Confinity co-founders.
