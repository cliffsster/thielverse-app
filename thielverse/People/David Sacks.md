> [!abstract] David O. Sacks
> [[PayPal]] COO, founder of Yammer and [[Craft Ventures]], co-host of the *All-In* podcast,
> and White House AI & Crypto Czar from 2025. The most politically operational node outside
> [[Peter Thiel]] himself.

## Key facts
- Born 1972 in Cape Town, South Africa. Stanford BA, University of Chicago JD.
- Editor-in-chief of the [[Stanford Review]]; co-wrote *The Diversity Myth* (1995) with [[Peter Thiel]].
- COO of [[PayPal]] and the driver of much of its product strategy through the eBay sale.
- Produced the film *Thank You for Smoking* (2005), founded Geni, then Yammer — sold to Microsoft for $1.2B in 2012. Interim CEO of Zenefits (2016–17).
- Founded [[Craft Ventures]] in 2017; co-hosts *All-In* with Chamath Palihapitiya, Jason Calacanis and David Friedberg — a significant political megaphone in tech.
- Named White House AI & Crypto Czar by [[Donald Trump]] in December 2024, a special-government-employee role shaping federal AI and digital-asset policy.
