> [!abstract] Scott Bessent
> Secretary of the Treasury since 2025; runs digital-asset policy alongside [[David Sacks]].

## Key facts
- Former chief investment officer for George Soros's fund and founder of Key Square Group — an unusual résumé for this administration.
- Confirmed in January 2025; leads on tariffs, tax policy and the strategic bitcoin reserve established by executive order.
- Treasury and Sacks's czar role jointly shaped the 2025 stablecoin and market-structure legislation that the crypto industry had sought for years.
- Treasury payment systems were among those [[Department of Government Efficiency]] staff sought access to, producing litigation.

## Connections
- [[David Sacks]] — crypto policy counterpart.
- [[Department of Government Efficiency]], [[Elon Musk]] — the Treasury systems access dispute.
- [[Donald Trump]] — appointing authority.
