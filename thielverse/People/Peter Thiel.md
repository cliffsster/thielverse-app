> [!abstract] Peter Thiel
> Co-founder and chairman of [[Palantir Technologies]]; co-founder and former CEO of [[PayPal]].
> The single highest-degree node in this graph — nearly every other person here connects to him
> within two hops.

## Key facts
- Born 1967 in Frankfurt, raised in Cleveland and South Africa. Stanford BA philosophy (1989), Stanford Law JD (1992).
- Founded [[Stanford Review]] in 1987 — the ideological seedbed for [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]], [[Joe Lonsdale]] and [[Blake Masters]].
- Co-founded [[Confinity]] in 1998 with [[Max Levchin]], [[Luke Nosek]] and [[Ken Howery]]. It merged with [[Elon Musk]]'s [[X.com]] in 2000 to become [[PayPal]]; eBay bought it for $1.5B in 2002.
- Made the first outside investment in Facebook ([[Meta Platforms]]) in 2004 — $500k for 10.2%, introduced by [[Sean Parker]]. Board member until 2022.
- Seeded [[Palantir Technologies]] in 2003 and remains its largest individual shareholder and chairman.
- Runs or co-founded [[Clarium Capital]], [[Founders Fund]], [[Mithril Capital]], [[Valar Ventures]], the [[Thiel Foundation]] and the [[Thiel Fellowship]].
- Spoke at the 2016 Republican National Convention, donated to and joined [[Donald Trump]]'s transition executive committee, then funded [[JD Vance]] ($15M) and [[Blake Masters]] in 2022 Senate races.
- Co-wrote *The Diversity Myth* (1995) with [[David Sacks]] and *Zero to One* (2014) with [[Blake Masters]].
