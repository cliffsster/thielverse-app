> [!abstract] Joi Ito
> Director of the [[MIT Media Lab]] 2011–2019; resigned after reporting revealed the lab had taken [[Jeffrey Epstein]]'s money and deliberately concealed it.

## Key facts
- Venture capitalist and academic; ran the Media Lab through its most prominent decade.
- Resigned in September 2019 after *The New Yorker* documented that the lab accepted Epstein donations after his conviction and recorded them as anonymous to hide the source.
- Used Epstein as a conduit to other wealthy donors — the mechanism by which Epstein bought proximity to elite science after 2008.
- [[Reid Hoffman]] was a Media Lab funder in this period and issued a public apology for his part in it.
