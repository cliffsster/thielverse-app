> [!abstract] Gilman Louie
> Founding CEO of [[In-Q-Tel]], the CIA's venture-capital arm — the person who built the
> mechanism that funded [[Palantir Technologies]] and made intelligence agencies startup
> customers.

## Key facts
- Began as a video-game developer; brought *Tetris* to the West through Spectrum HoloByte and Microprose.
- Founding CEO of [[In-Q-Tel]] from 1999 to 2006, creating the model of a government-funded venture vehicle that buys access to commercial technology rather than commissioning it.
- In-Q-Tel invested roughly $2m in Palantir around 2005 and connected it to CIA analysts — the validation that turned Palantir from a Thiel side bet into a real company.
- Later co-founded Alsop Louie Partners, served as a commissioner on the [[National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence]], and became CEO of America's Frontier Fund.

## Connections
- [[In-Q-Tel]] — the institution he built.
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Alex Karp]] — Palantir's founders, whose company In-Q-Tel legitimised.
- [[Central Intelligence Agency]] — In-Q-Tel's sponsor and Palantir's first customer.
- [[Eric Schmidt]] — NSCAI colleague.
