> [!abstract] Pam Bondi
> Attorney General since 2025, and the official whose handling of the [[Jeffrey Epstein]] files split [[Donald Trump]]'s coalition more sharply than any other single issue of the year.

## Key facts
- Florida Attorney General 2011–2019; confirmed as US Attorney General in February 2025.
- Oversaw a February 2025 "Phase 1" release widely criticised as containing almost nothing new, then a July 2025 Justice Department memo stating there was no Epstein "client list" — which triggered a furious backlash from the administration's own base.
- Congress passed the Epstein Files Transparency Act near-unanimously in November 2025, compelling releases her department had resisted.
- The episode is the clearest case of the coalition's populist wing turning on its own government.

## Connections
- [[Jeffrey Epstein]], [[Ghislaine Maxwell]] — the files and the custody decisions.
- [[Donald Trump]] — appointing authority and subject of the disclosure fight.
- [[Steve Bannon]], [[Tucker Carlson]], [[Nick Fuentes]] — the wing that revolted over the handling.
- [[Elon Musk]] — publicly invoked the files during his mid-2025 break with Trump.
