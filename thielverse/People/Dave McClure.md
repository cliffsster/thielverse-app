> Ran marketing at [[PayPal]], then founded 500 Startups — the highest-volume seed investor

## Key facts
- Director of marketing at PayPal, 2001–2004.
- Founded 500 Startups in 2010; it invested in thousands of companies across dozens of countries and trained a generation of seed investors.
- Resigned from 500 Startups in 2017 after admitting to sexual misconduct toward women in the startup community, following reporting in *The New York Times*; later started Practical Venture Capital.
