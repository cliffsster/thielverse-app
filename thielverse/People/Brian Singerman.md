> [!abstract] Brian Singerman
> Longtime general partner at [[Founders Fund]], responsible for some of its largest returns,
> who left to start his own vehicle with [[Peter Thiel]] backing.

## Key facts
- Former Google engineer; joined [[Founders Fund]] in 2008.
- Led or drove investments in Affirm, Oscar Health, Stemcentrx (sold to AbbVie for ~$10B), Wish and Forward.
- Left Founders Fund and launched GPx with Lee Linden in 2025 — a fund-of-funds-plus-co-invest structure, with Thiel reported as a principal backer.
