> [!abstract] Tucker Carlson
> Former Fox News host, now running the Tucker Carlson Network. The most-watched interviewer
> on the American right and the person who has pressed [[Peter Thiel]] and [[JD Vance]] more
> directly than anyone in this graph.

## Key facts
- Hosted *Tucker Carlson Tonight* 2016–2023, the highest-rated cable news show; fired by Fox in April 2023 in the wake of the Dominion settlement.
- Launched Tucker Carlson Network, with investment from [[1789 Capital]], Omeed Malik's fund.
- Close to [[JD Vance]] and among the loudest advocates for his selection as [[Donald Trump]]'s 2024 running mate.
- Interviewed [[Peter Thiel]] in 2025 in an exchange that became widely discussed — Thiel's long hesitation over whether he would prefer the human race to endure.
- Interviewed [[Nick Fuentes]] in October 2025, triggering the sharpest public rupture on the American right in years and a crisis at the [[Heritage Foundation]] under [[Kevin Roberts]].
- Fiercely anti-interventionist and openly hostile to [[AIPAC]] and to the pro-Israel wing of the Republican coalition — the fault line that now runs through this whole network.
