> [!abstract] Tulsi Gabbard
> Director of National Intelligence since 2025 — head of the intelligence community that has been [[Palantir Technologies]]' core customer base since [[In-Q-Tel]]'s 2005 investment.

## Key facts
- Democratic congresswoman from Hawaii 2013–2021 and 2020 presidential candidate; left the party in 2022 and endorsed Trump in 2024.
- Confirmed as DNI in February 2025 over objections about her past positions on surveillance and foreign policy.
- Oversees the 18-agency intelligence community, including the [[Central Intelligence Agency]] and [[National Security Agency]] — the agencies whose analysts co-developed Palantir's original product.
- A longtime critic of bulk surveillance now running the apparatus that conducts it, which makes her position genuinely hard to predict.
