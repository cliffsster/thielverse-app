> [!abstract] Geoff Lewis
> Former partner at [[Founders Fund]] and founder of [[Bedrock Capital]] — the route by which
> the Thiel network's capital reaches [[Flock Safety]].

## Key facts
- Joined [[Founders Fund]] in 2012 and spent roughly six years there, working on positions including Nubank and Wish.
- Founded [[Bedrock Capital]] in 2018 on a "narrative violation" thesis — backing companies that are mispriced because they contradict a consensus story.
- Bedrock has been an investor in [[Flock Safety]], the licence-plate-surveillance company, alongside [[Andreessen Horowitz]].

- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Founders Fund]] — training ground.
- [[Bedrock Capital]], [[Flock Safety]].
