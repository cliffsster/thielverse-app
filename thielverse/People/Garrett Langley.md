> [!abstract] Garrett Langley
> Founder and CEO of [[Flock Safety]], which has built the largest private
> licence-plate-reader network in the United States.

## Key facts
- Georgia Tech electrical engineering; founded Flock in Atlanta in 2017 after a neighbourhood break-in.
- Flock's cameras now operate in several thousand US communities under contracts with police departments and homeowners' associations.
- Argues publicly that Flock is a crime-solving tool with audit trails, not a surveillance system — an argument structurally identical to the one [[Alex Karp]] makes for [[Palantir Technologies]].
- Faced sustained scrutiny in 2025 over federal agency access to Flock data, including lookups on behalf of [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]].
