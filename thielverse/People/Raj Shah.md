> [!abstract] Raj Shah
> Former managing partner of the [[Defense Innovation Unit]] — the Pentagon office built to
> buy commercial technology, which made the [[Palantir Technologies]] and
> [[Anduril Industries]] sales motion possible at scale.

## Key facts
- F-16 pilot with combat tours in Iraq; co-founded and sold a cybersecurity company before government service.
- Led DIU (then DIUx) from 2016 to 2018, expanding its authority to award Other Transaction Agreements — contracting instruments that bypass traditional defence acquisition.
- Co-founded Shield Capital and became chairman and later CEO of Resilience, a cyber-insurance company.

## Connections
- [[Defense Innovation Unit]] — the office he ran.
- [[U.S. Department of Defense]] — the procurement system he changed from inside.
- [[Palantir Technologies]], [[Anduril Industries]] — principal beneficiaries of that change.
- [[Eric Schmidt]], [[Gilman Louie]] — the other architects of commercial-tech access to defence.
