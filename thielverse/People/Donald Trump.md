## Key facts
- [[Peter Thiel]] endorsed him at the 2016 Republican National Convention — a genuine break with Silicon Valley consensus at the time — donated $1.25m, and joined the transition executive committee.
- Thiel-network figures placed in the first administration included [[Michael Kratsios]] as US CTO and [[Ken Howery]] as Ambassador to Sweden; [[Trae Stephens]] served on the DoD transition team.
- Selected [[JD Vance]] as running mate in July 2024, putting a Thiel protégé a heartbeat from the presidency.
- Second-term appointments from this network: [[David Sacks]] as AI & Crypto Czar, [[Michael Kratsios]] at OSTP, [[Jim O'Neill]] as HHS deputy secretary, [[Ken Howery]] as Ambassador to Denmark, and [[Elon Musk]] leading the [[Department of Government Efficiency]].
- Musk broke with him publicly in mid-2025 after leaving DOGE.
