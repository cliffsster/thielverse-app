> [!abstract] Marc Andreessen
> Co-founder of Netscape and of [[Andreessen Horowitz]]; author of the *Techno-Optimist
> Manifesto*. The other centre of gravity in tech's rightward turn — a peer of
> [[Peter Thiel]] rather than a product of him.

## Key facts
- Wrote Mosaic, co-founded Netscape (1994), then founded a16z with Ben Horowitz in 2009.
- On the Facebook/[[Meta Platforms]] board since 2008 — one of the few people with a seat at both the Zuckerberg and Thiel tables.
- Published the *Techno-Optimist Manifesto* (2023), naming "the enemy" as safetyism, sustainability, trust-and-safety and "existential risk" — the explicit anti-[[Anthropic]] position.
- Backed [[JD Vance]]'s [[Narya Capital]] alongside Thiel and [[Eric Schmidt]].
- Endorsed [[Donald Trump]] in 2024, advised the transition on personnel, and pushed the "Little Tech" agenda against AI regulation.
- a16z's American Dynamism practice is the main capital rival to [[Founders Fund]] in defence tech, backing [[Anduril Industries]] and [[Flock Safety]].
