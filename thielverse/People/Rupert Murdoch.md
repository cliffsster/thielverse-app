> [!abstract] Rupert Murdoch
> Founder of the Fox media empire — [[Tucker Carlson]]'s employer until 2023, and the incumbent right-wing media power that the newer network media described in this vault is competing against.

## Key facts
- Built News Corp and Fox News into the dominant conservative media institution in the US.
- [[Fox News]] settled the Dominion Voting Systems defamation suit for $787.5m in April 2023; Carlson was fired days later.
- Carlson's independent network, [[Bari Weiss]]'s move to CBS, and podcast media like [[All-In Podcast]] all fragment the audience Fox consolidated.
- Succession disputes over the family trust ran through 2024–25.
