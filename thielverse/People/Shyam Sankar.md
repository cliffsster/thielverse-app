> [!abstract] Shyam Sankar
> CTO and EVP of [[Palantir Technologies]]; joined in 2006 as one of the first forward-deployed
> engineers and became the public voice of the company's defence-industrial argument.

## Key facts
- Born in Nigeria to Indian parents, raised in Florida. Cornell EE, Stanford MS management science.
- Employee number ~13; built out the forward-deployed engineer model that put Palantir staff inside customer agencies.
- Ran Palantir's business operations before becoming CTO in 2019.
- Author of *The Defense Reformation* (2024), an 18-point critique of US defence procurement widely circulated in Washington; hosts the *Moment of Zen* podcast.
- The most-quoted Palantir executive on the argument that primes should be displaced by software companies.
