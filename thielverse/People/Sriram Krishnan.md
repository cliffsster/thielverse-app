> [!abstract] Sriram Krishnan
> Former general partner at [[Andreessen Horowitz]]; Senior Policy Adviser for AI at the [[Office of Science and Technology Policy]] since 2025 — the a16z counterpart to [[David Sacks]].

## Key facts
- Product and engineering roles at Microsoft, Facebook, Snap and Twitter before joining a16z, where he led crypto and consumer investments.
- Named senior AI policy adviser in December 2024, working under [[Michael Kratsios]] at OSTP and alongside Sacks.
- His appointment triggered the December 2024 H-1B fight, when [[Steve Bannon]] and the populist right attacked the tech wing over high-skilled immigration.
- With Sacks, Kratsios and [[Scott Kupor]], completes a set of four senior federal technology appointments drawn from this network's two principal venture firms.
