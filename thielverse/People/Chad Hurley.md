> [!abstract] Chad Hurley
> [[PayPal]] designer — he designed the original PayPal logo — and co-founder and first CEO
> of [[YouTube]].

## Key facts
- Indiana University of Pennsylvania fine arts; hired at [[Confinity]] as its first designer after an interview with [[Peter Thiel]].
- Co-founded [[YouTube]] in February 2005 with [[Steve Chen]] and [[Jawed Karim]]; Google bought it for $1.65B in November 2006.
- Seed funding came from [[Roelof Botha]] at [[Sequoia Capital]] — a PayPal alum funding PayPal alumni.
- Later co-founded MixBit and became a part-owner of the Golden State Warriors and a Formula 1 investor.

## Connections
- [[Steve Chen]], [[Jawed Karim]] — YouTube co-founders, both ex-PayPal.
- [[Roelof Botha]] — seed investor.
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Max Levchin]] — hired him at Confinity.
