> [!abstract] Keir Starmer
> Prime Minister of the United Kingdom since July 2024; inherited and continued the [[NHS England]] contract with [[Palantir Technologies]].

## Key facts
- Labour leader; took office with the £330m NHS Federated Data Platform contract already signed by the previous government, and did not cancel it.
- Signed the UK–US Tech Prosperity Deal in September 2025, bringing large US AI infrastructure commitments to Britain.
- His government's continuation of the Palantir contract removed the partisan framing from the NHS dispute and left the objections purely about data governance.
- [[Louis Mosley]] runs the Palantir side of that relationship.
