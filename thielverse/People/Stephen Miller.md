> [!abstract] Stephen Miller
> Deputy Chief of Staff for Policy and Homeland Security Adviser; the architect of the immigration enforcement programme that drives [[Palantir Technologies]]' fastest-growing contract line.

## Key facts
- Trump's senior policy adviser across both terms; founded America First Legal in between.
- Set the 2025 interior-enforcement targets — reported arrest quotas, expanded expedited removal, third-country deportations — that expanded ICE's operational tempo and its data-system requirements.
- Negotiated deportation arrangements with third countries including El Salvador under [[Nayib Bukele]].
- The demand side of the Palantir ICE relationship: the policy generates the requirement the software fulfils.
