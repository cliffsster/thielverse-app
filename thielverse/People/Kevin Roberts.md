> [!abstract] Kevin Roberts
> President of the [[Heritage Foundation]] and the public face of Project 2025 — whose
> handling of the [[Tucker Carlson]]/[[Nick Fuentes]] interview became the right's biggest
> institutional crisis of 2025.

## Key facts
- Became Heritage president in 2021 and reoriented it from conventional conservatism toward national conservatism.
- Oversaw Project 2025, the 900-page governing blueprint whose personnel and deregulatory recommendations track closely with the second Trump administration's actions.
- Released a video in late 2025 defending Carlson after the Fuentes interview, describing critics as a "venomous coalition"; the backlash produced donor losses, staff resignations and a partial retraction.

- [[Heritage Foundation]] — president.
- [[Tucker Carlson]], [[Nick Fuentes]] — the 2025 rupture.
