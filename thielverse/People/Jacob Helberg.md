> **The single strongest Palantir-to-cabinet-department edge in this graph.**

- Ran global policy for Google before joining Palantir as an adviser to its CEO.
- Commissioner on the US–China Economic and Security Review Commission; a principal architect of the argument that the US is in a technological cold war with China — and one of the loudest advocates of the TikTok divestiture law.
- A significant Republican donor and fundraiser in the 2024 cycle.
- Confirmed to the State Department role in 2025, putting a serving Palantir adviser in charge of the department's economic-statecraft and technology portfolio.
