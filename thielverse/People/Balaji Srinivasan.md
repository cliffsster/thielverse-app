> [!abstract] Balaji Srinivasan
> Former general partner at [[Andreessen Horowitz]] and CTO of Coinbase; author of
> *The Network State*. The most systematic living statement of the exit-over-voice politics
> [[Peter Thiel]] has funded for two decades.

## Key facts
- Stanford PhD; co-founded Counsyl (genetic testing) and Earn.com (sold to Coinbase).
- General partner at a16z, then Coinbase CTO; was reportedly considered for FDA commissioner in 2017 — the same slot [[Jim O'Neill]] was floated for.
- Published *The Network State* (2022), arguing that online communities should accumulate capital and territory until they achieve diplomatic recognition — a direct descendant of Thiel-funded seasteading.
- Lost a widely publicised $1m bet on Bitcoin hyperinflation in 2023.
