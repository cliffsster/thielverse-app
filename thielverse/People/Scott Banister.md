> [!abstract] Scott Banister
> Early [[PayPal]] adviser and board member, founder of IronPort, and a prolific angel investor.

## Key facts
- University of Illinois, part of the [[Max Levchin]] Illinois cluster; dropped out to work on early internet advertising ideas (he is credited with an early keyword-advertising concept later commercialised as pay-per-click).
- Adviser and board member at PayPal in its formative years.
- Founded IronPort Systems, an email-security company Cisco bought for $830m in 2007.
- Angel investor in Zappos, Uber, [[Affirm]] and many others; married to [[Cyan Banister]].

## Connections
- [[Max Levchin]] — Illinois connection and PayPal introduction.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — PayPal board relationship.
- [[Cyan Banister]] — spouse and fellow investor, later a partner at [[Founders Fund]].
