> [!abstract] Nick Fuentes
> White-nationalist streamer and leader of the "America First"/groyper movement. Present in
> this graph as an ambient force in the online right, **not** as part of the Thiel network.

## Key facts
- Born 1998; attended the 2017 Charlottesville rally; built an audience through livestreaming after being deplatformed by most major services.
- Dined with [[Donald Trump]] at Mar-a-Lago in November 2022 alongside Ye, a meeting Trump said he had not arranged and which drew broad Republican condemnation.
- His X account was reinstated in 2024 under [[Elon Musk]]'s ownership.
- [[Tucker Carlson]]'s October 2025 interview with him split the American right, drew a defence from [[Heritage Foundation]] president [[Kevin Roberts]], and led to sustained internal fallout at Heritage.
- Explicitly opposed to [[AIPAC]] and to the pro-Israel Republican establishment.

- [[Heritage Foundation]], [[Kevin Roberts]] — the institution the interview fractured.
