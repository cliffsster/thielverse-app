> [!abstract] Lina Khan
> Chair of the [[Federal Trade Commission]] 2021–2025 and the most sustained antagonist this network has faced from inside the state.

## Key facts
- Wrote "Amazon's Antitrust Paradox" as a Yale law student in 2017, reframing US antitrust away from the consumer-welfare standard.
- As FTC chair, brought monopolisation cases against Amazon and Meta, blocked or challenged acquisitions, and banned most non-compete agreements.
- [[Marc Andreessen]], [[David Sacks]] and [[Elon Musk]] all attacked her publicly; opposition to her tenure was a significant motivator in tech's 2024 rightward shift and its donations.
- Replaced under the second Trump administration by a chair who kept several big-tech cases running while dropping the broader Khan agenda.
