> [!abstract] David Ellison
> Chairman and CEO of [[Paramount Skydance]]; acquired CBS and then [[The Free Press]], installing [[Bari Weiss]] atop CBS News.

## Key facts
- Founded Skydance Media in 2010; completed the merger with Paramount Global in August 2025, backed by his father [[Larry Ellison]].
- Bought The Free Press in October 2025 and made Weiss Editor-in-Chief of CBS News — a change in editorial direction, not just ownership.
- Pursued Warner Bros. Discovery in the subsequent consolidation wave.
- His acquisitions are the mechanism by which a heterodox-media property became a network news operation.
