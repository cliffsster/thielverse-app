> [!abstract] Jeff Bezos
> Amazon founder, *Washington Post* owner, and — through Amazon's roughly $8B investment in [[Anthropic]] — the largest single financial backer of the AI lab that partners with [[Palantir Technologies]].

## Key facts
- Founded Amazon in 1994; owner of the *Washington Post* since 2013; founded Blue Origin, [[SpaceX]]'s principal commercial rival.
- Amazon is Anthropic's largest investor and its primary compute provider — the AWS side of the Anthropic–Palantir defence partnership.
- Blocked the *Washington Post*'s planned 2024 Harris endorsement and restructured its opinion section in 2025 around "personal liberties and free markets," prompting resignations.
- Moved from Trump antagonist in the first term to visible accommodation in the second.
