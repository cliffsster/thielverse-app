> [!abstract] Mark Zuckerberg
> Founder and CEO of [[Meta Platforms]]. Not a PayPal alum, but the node that made
> [[Peter Thiel]]'s fortune and kept him on a public-company board for 17 years.

## Key facts
- Took Thiel's $500,000 convertible note in August 2004 for 10.2% of Facebook — introduced by [[Sean Parker]]. It became roughly $1B by the 2012 IPO.
- Thiel sat on the Facebook/Meta board from 2005 until stepping down in February 2022, in part to focus on political spending.
- Meta acquired Oculus in 2014 for ~$2B, bringing [[Palmer Luckey]] into the company — and firing him in 2017 after his political donations became public.
- Hired [[Yishan Wong]] and other PayPal engineers during Facebook's scaling years.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — first outside investor and longest-serving board member.
- [[Sean Parker]] — founding president and the Thiel introduction.
- [[Palmer Luckey]] — acquired, then dismissed; the dismissal directly precipitated [[Anduril Industries]].
- [[Reid Hoffman]] — early angel investor.
