> [!abstract] Russel Simmons
> [[PayPal]] engineer and co-founder and first CTO of [[Yelp]].

## Key facts
- Engineer at PayPal working alongside [[Jeremy Stoppelman]].
- Co-founded [[Yelp]] in 2004 and served as CTO until 2010; built the original review platform.
- Later worked as an angel investor and adviser; not related to the Def Jam founder of the same name.

## Connections
- [[Jeremy Stoppelman]] — PayPal colleague and Yelp co-founder.
- [[Max Levchin]] — seed funder of Yelp.
- [[Yelp]], [[PayPal]].
