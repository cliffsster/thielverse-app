> [!abstract] Russ Vought
> Director of the Office of Management and Budget; a principal author of Project 2025 and the most effective executor of its civil-service programme.

## Key facts
- OMB director in the first Trump administration; returned in 2025 after running the Center for Renewing America.
- Wrote the Project 2025 chapter on executive power and has long argued for reviving impoundment — refusing to spend appropriated funds — as a presidential authority.
- Directed the effective shutdown of the [[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau]] and drove the reduction-in-force machinery alongside the [[Department of Government Efficiency]].
- Where [[Elon Musk]]'s DOGE was loud and short-lived, Vought's version is procedural, durable and still running.
