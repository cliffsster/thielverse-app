> [!abstract] Brian Schimpf
> [[Palantir Technologies]] engineering leader turned co-founder and CEO of
> [[Anduril Industries]].

## Key facts
- Cornell engineering; spent roughly a decade at Palantir, rising to Director of Engineering.
- Ran the teams that deployed Palantir software into defence and intelligence customers.
- Co-founded Anduril in 2017 and has been CEO throughout its growth into border surveillance towers, counter-drone systems, Fury autonomous fighters and the Lattice software layer.
- Represents the operating half of Anduril, against [[Palmer Luckey]]'s public-facing half.
