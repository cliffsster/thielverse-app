> [!abstract] Joe Lonsdale
> Co-founder of [[Palantir Technologies]] at 21, then [[Addepar]], [[OpenGov]] and the venture
> firm [[8VC]]. The most institution-building member of the founding five.

## Key facts
- Born 1982. Stanford CS; editor-in-chief of the [[Stanford Review]] — the direct pipeline to [[Peter Thiel]].
- Interned at [[PayPal]] under Thiel, then worked as an early analyst at [[Clarium Capital]].
- Co-founded Palantir in 2003–04 with Thiel, [[Alex Karp]], [[Stephen Cohen]] and [[Nathan Gettings]]; left day-to-day involvement in 2009.
- Founded [[Addepar]] (wealth-management data, 2009) and [[OpenGov]] (government finance software, 2012); founded [[8VC]] in 2015.
- Founded the [[Cicero Institute]] (2016), a state-policy think tank, and co-founded the [[University of Austin]] (2021).
- Moved 8VC from the Bay Area to Austin in 2020 and became a prominent booster of the Texas tech migration.
- A major Republican donor and one of the loudest advocates of the "American Dynamism" defence-tech thesis.
