> [!abstract] Nathan Gettings
> [[PayPal]] engineer, co-founder and first CTO of [[Palantir Technologies]], later a co-founder
> of [[Affirm]]. The literal technical bridge between the two companies.

## Key facts
- Worked on fraud detection engineering at [[PayPal]] — the system Palantir was explicitly modelled on.
- Co-founded Palantir in 2003 and served as its first CTO, building the early Gotham architecture.
- Left Palantir and co-founded [[Affirm]] in 2012 with [[Max Levchin]], Alex Rampell and Jeff Kaditz.
- Later active as an angel investor and technical co-founder; keeps a very low public profile.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — PayPal boss who recruited him into Palantir.
- [[Max Levchin]] — PayPal CTO and later Affirm co-founder; the strongest PayPal→Palantir→Affirm edge in the graph.
- [[Alex Karp]], [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Stephen Cohen]] — Palantir co-founders.
- [[PayPal]] — where the anti-fraud technique that became Palantir's core product originated.
