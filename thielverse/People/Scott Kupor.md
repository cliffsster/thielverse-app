> [!abstract] Scott Kupor
> Founding partner and long-time managing partner of [[Andreessen Horowitz]]; Director of the [[Office of Personnel Management]] since 2025 — the agency that runs the federal civil service.

## Key facts
- a16z's first employee and managing partner for over a decade; author of *Secrets of Sand Hill Road*.
- Confirmed as OPM Director in July 2025, inheriting an agency reshaped by the [[Department of Government Efficiency]]'s mass workforce reductions.
- Places a venture-capital operator in charge of federal hiring, classification and workforce rules — the machinery [[Curtis Yarvin]]'s "retire all government employees" argument targets, and that Project 2025 proposed to rewrite.
