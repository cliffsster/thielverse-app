> [!abstract] Larry Ellison
> Oracle co-founder and one of the wealthiest people alive; through his son [[David Ellison]] he now sits behind CBS News, and through Oracle he sits inside the TikTok US structure and the Stargate AI build-out.

## Key facts
- Co-founded Oracle in 1977; longtime [[Elon Musk]] ally and a Tesla board member from 2018 to 2022.
- Financed his son's Skydance acquisition of Paramount, creating [[Paramount Skydance]] in 2025.
- Oracle is a lead partner in Stargate, the AI-infrastructure venture with [[OpenAI]] and SoftBank, and took a stake in the 2025 TikTok US joint venture.
- Combines media ownership, cloud infrastructure for frontier AI, and federal contracting in a single position — a concentration with few parallels.
