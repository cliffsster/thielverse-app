> [!abstract] Chamath Palihapitiya
> Founder of Social Capital, former Facebook executive, and co-host of *All-In* with
> [[David Sacks]] — a significant amplifier for this network's politics.

## Key facts
- Early Facebook VP of growth; founded Social Capital in 2011.
- Drove the 2020–21 SPAC boom, taking Virgin Galactic, Opendoor and Clover Health public; several performed badly and drew SEC and shareholder scrutiny.
- *All-In*, with Sacks, Jason Calacanis and David Friedberg, became one of the most influential political podcasts in tech and a staging ground for the 2024 rightward shift.
- Hosted a major Trump fundraiser in 2024 with Sacks.
