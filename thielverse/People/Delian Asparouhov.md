> [!abstract] Delian Asparouhov
> Partner at [[Founders Fund]] and co-founder of Varda Space Industries — part of the
> generation of the network that never worked at PayPal.

## Key facts
- Born in Bulgaria, raised in the US; MIT dropout and a [[Thiel Fellowship]]-adjacent figure in the network's younger cohort.
- Joined [[Founders Fund]] as a principal and became a partner, focusing on space, defence and health.
- Co-founded Varda Space Industries in 2020 to manufacture pharmaceuticals in orbit and return them via re-entry capsules.
- A prolific and combative poster on X, functioning as one of the network's public messaging channels.
