> [!abstract] Keith Rabois
> [[PayPal]] EVP for business development, then an executive at [[LinkedIn]], Slide and Square,
> and a partner at Khosla Ventures and [[Founders Fund]]. The network's most prolific
> talent-spotter.

## Key facts
- Born 1969. Stanford BA (where he wrote for the [[Stanford Review]]), Harvard Law JD.
- Ran PayPal's business development — including the eBay integration strategy that made the acquisition inevitable.
- Executive roles at [[LinkedIn]] (with [[Reid Hoffman]]), Slide (with [[Max Levchin]]) and COO of Square under Jack Dorsey.
- Partner at Khosla Ventures, then [[Founders Fund]] (2019–2024), then back to Khosla in 2024.
- Early investor in Airbnb, Stripe, Faire, Ramp and [[YouTube]]; co-founded OpenStore in 2021.
- Led the high-profile "tech moves to Miami" push in 2020–21.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — Stanford Review, PayPal, and later Founders Fund partner.
- [[David Sacks]], [[Joe Lonsdale]], [[Blake Masters]] — Stanford Review alumni.
- [[Reid Hoffman]], [[Max Levchin]] — PayPal colleagues who later hired him.
- [[Roelof Botha]], [[Jeremy Stoppelman]] — PayPal cohort.
- [[Founders Fund]] — partner for five years.
