> [!abstract] Cyan Banister
> The first female partner at [[Founders Fund]] and an early investor in SpaceX, Uber and
> Postmates.

## Key facts
- Left home as a teenager and was homeless before teaching herself technology — the least conventional background in this graph.
- Angel investor with [[Scott Banister]] before joining [[Founders Fund]] as a partner in 2016.
- Made early personal investments in [[SpaceX]], Uber, Postmates, Deepgram and Niantic.
- Left Founders Fund to co-found Long Journey Ventures in 2021.

- [[Scott Banister]] — spouse and co-investor.
