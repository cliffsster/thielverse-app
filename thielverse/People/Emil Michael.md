> Former Uber chief business officer; Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering since 2025 — the Pentagon's chief technology role, and the seat [[Michael Kratsios]] held in an acting capacity in 2020.

## Key facts
- Uber's senior business executive during its aggressive growth years; left in 2017.
- Confirmed as USD(R&E) in 2025, overseeing Pentagon research, prototyping and the technology-transition pipeline.
- The office sits directly upstream of the programmes [[Palantir Technologies]] and [[Anduril Industries]] compete for, and of the [[Defense Innovation Unit]].
