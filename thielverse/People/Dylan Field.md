> [!abstract] Dylan Field
> Co-founder and CEO of Figma and a 2012 [[Thiel Fellowship]] recipient.

## Key facts
- Left Brown University on a Thiel Fellowship to found Figma with Evan Wallace in 2012.
- Built browser-based collaborative design tooling that became the industry default.
- Adobe agreed to buy Figma for $20B in 2022; regulators in the UK and EU blocked it and the deal was abandoned in 2023 with a $1B break fee. Figma went public in 2025.
