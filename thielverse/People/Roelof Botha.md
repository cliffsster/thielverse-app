> [!abstract] Roelof Botha
> [[PayPal]] CFO at 28, then a partner and eventually senior steward of [[Sequoia Capital]] —
> the PayPal alum who ended up running the most established firm in venture capital.

## Key facts
- Born 1973 in Pretoria, South Africa; grandson of former South African foreign minister Pik Botha. Stanford MBA.
- Joined PayPal in 2000, became CFO in 2001, and took the company through its IPO and the eBay sale.
- Joined [[Sequoia Capital]] in 2003; led its investments in [[YouTube]], Instagram, Square, Unity and MongoDB.
- Became senior steward (global head) of Sequoia in 2022.
- Backed [[Chad Hurley]], [[Steve Chen]] and [[Jawed Karim]]'s [[YouTube]] — one PayPal alum funding three others.

- [[Elon Musk]], [[Peter Thiel]], [[Max Levchin]] — PayPal leadership cohort.
