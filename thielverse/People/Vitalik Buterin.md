> [!abstract] Vitalik Buterin
> Creator of Ethereum and a 2014 [[Thiel Fellowship]] recipient — the fellowship's most
> commonly cited success.

## Key facts
- Born 1994 in Russia, raised in Canada. Left the University of Waterloo after receiving a $100,000 Thiel Fellowship grant in 2014.
- Published the Ethereum whitepaper in 2013; the network launched in 2015 and became the base layer for smart contracts and most of crypto's application ecosystem.
- Politically and philosophically distant from most of the network — his writing on pluralism and "d/acc" is explicitly a counter-argument to centralised-power techno-politics.
