> [!abstract] Gail Slater
> Assistant Attorney General for the [[DOJ Antitrust Division]] since 2025 — and, before that, economic policy adviser to Senator [[JD Vance]]. The clearest Vance-to-regulator edge in the graph.

## Key facts
- FTC attorney, then a technology policy adviser in the first Trump administration's National Economic Council, then counsel to Vance in the Senate.
- Nominated by [[Donald Trump]] in December 2024 and confirmed in 2025, with Vance's explicit backing.
- Continued the Google search and ad-tech remedies and other big-tech enforcement — evidence that the "populist antitrust" strand survived into an administration otherwise staffed by the industry.
- Her position is the sharpest internal contradiction in this network's politics: the same coalition holds both the deregulation portfolio and an active antitrust programme.
