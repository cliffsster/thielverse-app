> [!abstract] Howard Lutnick
> Secretary of Commerce since 2025 — the department that controls AI chip export policy and brokered the TikTok US restructuring.

## Key facts
- CEO of Cantor Fitzgerald; co-chaired the 2024 Trump transition, giving him unusual influence over personnel.
- Commerce runs the Bureau of Industry and Security, which sets semiconductor export controls — the single biggest lever over the AI industry's China exposure.
- Central to the 2025 TikTok US joint venture involving Oracle and [[Larry Ellison]].
- Oversaw the reworking of CHIPS Act awards into equity-style arrangements.
