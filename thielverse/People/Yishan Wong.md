> [!abstract] Yishan Wong
> [[PayPal]] engineer, early Facebook engineering director, and CEO of Reddit 2012–2014.

## Key facts
- Carnegie Mellon; engineer at PayPal in the early 2000s.
- Director of engineering at Facebook ([[Meta Platforms]]) during its scaling years.
- CEO of Reddit from 2012 to 2014; resigned in a dispute over office relocation, shortly before the site's largest moderation crises.
- Founded Terraformation in 2019, a reforestation company — an unusual pivot for the cohort.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Max Levchin]] — PayPal-era leadership.
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]] — Facebook engineering leadership under him.
- [[Sam Altman]] — Reddit's board and Y Combinator orbit overlapped during his tenure.
