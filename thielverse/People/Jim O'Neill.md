> [!abstract] Jim O'Neill
> Longtime [[Peter Thiel]] associate — Clarium, [[Mithril Capital]], the Thiel-backed SENS
> Research Foundation — and Deputy Secretary of Health and Human Services from 2025.

## Key facts
- Yale BA, University of Chicago; served in HHS under George W. Bush as principal associate deputy secretary.
- Worked with Thiel across [[Clarium Capital]] and as a managing director at [[Mithril Capital]]; CEO of the Thiel-funded SENS Research Foundation, which works on anti-ageing research.
- Thiel reportedly pushed for his appointment to run the FDA in 2017; the nomination did not proceed. O'Neill had argued publicly for approving drugs on safety alone, without proof of efficacy.
- Confirmed as Deputy Secretary of HHS in 2025 under Robert F. Kennedy Jr., and additionally named acting director of the CDC in August 2025 after the removal of Susan Monarez.
