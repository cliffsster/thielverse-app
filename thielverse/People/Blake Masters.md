> [!abstract] Blake Masters
> Co-author of *Zero to One*, former COO of Thiel Capital and president of the
> [[Thiel Foundation]], and the 2022 Republican Senate nominee in Arizona.

## Key facts
- Born 1986 in Tucson. Stanford BA and Stanford Law.
- Took the notes in [[Peter Thiel]]'s 2012 Stanford CS183 startup class that circulated online and became the book *Zero to One* (2014), co-credited to both.
- Wrote for the [[Stanford Review]], following the same path as [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]] and [[Joe Lonsdale]].
- COO of Thiel Capital and president of the [[Thiel Foundation]], where he ran the [[Thiel Fellowship]].
- Won the 2022 Arizona Republican Senate primary with roughly $15m of Thiel money, then lost the general election to Mark Kelly. Lost a House primary in 2024.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — student, co-author, employee and funded candidate; the most complete version of the Thiel protégé pipeline.
- [[JD Vance]] — the parallel 2022 Thiel-funded Senate candidacy that succeeded.
- [[Thiel Foundation]], [[Thiel Fellowship]] — organisations he ran.
- [[Jack Selby]] — fellow Thiel operator with Arizona ties.
- [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]], [[Joe Lonsdale]] — [[Stanford Review]] lineage.
