> [!abstract] John Ratcliffe
> Director of the [[Central Intelligence Agency]] since 2025 — the agency whose venture arm made [[Palantir Technologies]] a real company.

## Key facts
- Texas congressman, then Director of National Intelligence 2020–2021; confirmed as CIA Director in January 2025.
- Runs the agency that was Palantir's first customer and, through [[In-Q-Tel]], its first institutional investor.
- Has pushed expanded covert action and a more aggressive posture in the Western Hemisphere.
