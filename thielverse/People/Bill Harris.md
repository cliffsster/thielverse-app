> [!abstract] Bill Harris
> The first CEO of the merged [[X.com]]/[[Confinity]] entity, brought in for adult supervision
> and pushed out within months.

## Key facts
- Former CEO of Intuit; recruited in 1999 to run X.com.
- Negotiated the [[X.com]]–[[Confinity]] merger in March 2000, then resigned in May 2000 amid conflict between the two founding camps — leaving [[Elon Musk]] as CEO, and then [[Peter Thiel]] after Musk's own ouster in September.
- Later founded Personal Capital, sold to Empower for roughly $1B in 2020, and Nirvana Money.

## Connections
- [[Elon Musk]], [[Peter Thiel]], [[Max Levchin]] — the three-way succession he sat at the start of.
- [[PayPal]], [[X.com]], [[Confinity]].
