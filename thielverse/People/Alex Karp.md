> [!abstract] Alexander C. Karp
> Co-founder and CEO of [[Palantir Technologies]] since 2004. A self-described progressive
> running the most controversial defence software company in the world — the deliberate
> counterweight to [[Peter Thiel]] at the top of the company.

## Key facts
- Born 1967 in New York; father a paediatrician, mother an artist. Haverford BA, Stanford Law JD (1992).
- Left law for a doctorate in neoclassical social theory at Goethe University Frankfurt (2002), in the Frankfurt School / Habermas tradition. He is usually called "Dr. Karp" internally.
- Ran the Caedmon Group, a London money-management firm, before Palantir — which is how Thiel knew he could handle investors.
- Has no engineering background; positions himself as the company's philosopher and salesman while [[Stephen Cohen]] and [[Shyam Sankar]] run product.
- Took Palantir public by direct listing on the NYSE (PLTR) on 30 September 2020.
- Publicly defends selling to Western militaries and intelligence agencies while refusing work in China; wrote *The Technological Republic* (2025) with Nicholas Zamiska.
- Politically a registered Democrat who has criticised his own party and Silicon Valley peers; an unusual profile inside a Thiel-founded company.
