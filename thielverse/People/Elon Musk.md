> [!abstract] Elon Musk
> Founder of [[X.com]], which merged with [[Confinity]] to form [[PayPal]]; later founder of
> [[SpaceX]], Tesla, Neuralink, The Boring Company and xAI, and owner of Twitter/X.
> Briefly the most powerful unelected figure in the US government in 2025.

## Key facts
- Born 1971 in Pretoria, South Africa. Sold Zip2 in 1999 and put the proceeds into [[X.com]], an online bank.
- X.com merged with [[Confinity]] in March 2000. Musk was ousted as CEO that September in favour of [[Peter Thiel]] — a genuine falling-out that both later moved past.
- Remained PayPal's largest shareholder at the 2002 eBay sale, netting roughly $180m, which funded [[SpaceX]] (2002) and Tesla.
- [[Founders Fund]] and [[Luke Nosek]] made an early and enormously profitable investment in SpaceX.
- Bought Twitter in 2022 for $44B and renamed it X.
- Led the [[Department of Government Efficiency]] in 2025 after backing [[Donald Trump]]'s campaign with over $250m, departed in May 2025, and then broke with Trump publicly.
