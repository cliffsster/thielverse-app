> [!abstract] Pete Hegseth
> Secretary of Defense since 2025 — head of the department that is [[Palantir Technologies]]' and [[Anduril Industries]]' largest customer.

## Key facts
- Army National Guard veteran and Fox News host before nomination; confirmed in January 2025 on a tie-breaking vote by [[JD Vance]].
- Oversaw the department's renaming to the Department of War and a push to accelerate software and commercial-technology acquisition — the reform [[Shyam Sankar]] and [[Christian Brose]] had argued for from the outside.
- The Pentagon under his tenure expanded Palantir's Army programmes and handed [[Anduril Industries]] the IVAS soldier-headset programme.
