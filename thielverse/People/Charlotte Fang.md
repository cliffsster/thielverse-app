> [!abstract] Charlotte Fang
> Founder of [[Remilia Corporation]], the collective behind the Milady Maker NFT project.
> Connected to this graph culturally and through one [[Elon Musk]] post — not financially.

## Key facts
- Founded Remilia and launched Milady Maker in 2021, a 10,000-piece pixel-art NFT collection built around a deliberately abrasive "network spirituality" aesthetic.
- Stepped back from the project in 2022 after being identified as the operator of a previous pseudonymous account associated with a harmful online community, then returned.
- Remilia's aesthetic became a dominant visual language for a strand of extremely online right-adjacent internet culture — overlapping in audience, though not organisation, with the [[Curtis Yarvin]] milieu.
