> [!abstract] Matt Grimm
> Co-founder and Chief Operating Officer of [[Anduril Industries]]; previously ran business
> operations at [[Palantir Technologies]].

## Key facts
- Spent years at Palantir on the operations and contracts side, learning how to sell software into US defence budgets.
- Co-founded Anduril in 2017 and built its manufacturing, supply-chain and contracting functions — the parts of a hardware company Palantir alumni had not previously had to build.
