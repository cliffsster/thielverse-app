> [!abstract] Benjamin Netanyahu
> Prime Minister of Israel; leads the government whose defence ministry signed a strategic partnership with [[Palantir Technologies]] in January 2024.

## Key facts
- Israel's longest-serving prime minister, in office since 2022 in the current term.
- The [[Israel Ministry of Defense]] partnership with Palantir was announced weeks into the Gaza war, when most technology companies were avoiding any public position.
- [[Alex Karp]] has been unusually outspoken in support of Israel, holding a Palantir board meeting in Tel Aviv in January 2024.
- His government is therefore the direct counterparty to the position that puts Palantir on the opposite side of the American right's anti-interventionist wing.
