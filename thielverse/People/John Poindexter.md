> [!abstract] John Poindexter
> Reagan's National Security Adviser, Iran-Contra figure, and director of DARPA's Total
> Information Awareness programme — the surveillance architecture [[Palantir Technologies]]
> is most often described as the commercial successor to.

## Key facts
- US Navy vice admiral; National Security Adviser to Ronald Reagan 1985–86. Convicted on five Iran-Contra counts in 1990; the convictions were overturned on appeal in 1991 on immunity grounds.
- Ran DARPA's Information Awareness Office from 2002, whose Total Information Awareness programme aimed to fuse commercial and government data to detect terrorist activity in advance.
- Congress defunded TIA in 2003 after a public backlash; components of the work reportedly continued under other names inside the intelligence community.
- Palantir was founded in the same window with a nearly identical technical premise but an explicit privacy-and-audit layer — a design choice its founders cite as the difference. Reporting has described Poindexter as an early informal contact for the company; this is journalistic account rather than confirmed record.
