> [!abstract] Garry Tan
> President and CEO of [[Y Combinator]] since 2023, and a central figure in San Francisco's
> tech-backed political turn.

## Key facts
- Co-founded Posterous, became a YC partner, then co-founded Initialized Capital (an early Coinbase and Instacart investor).
- Took over YC in 2023 and refocused it on AI and on lobbying, opening a Washington policy operation.
- Bankrolled GrowSF and other moderate-slate efforts that reshaped San Francisco's Board of Supervisors — the local-politics version of what this network does nationally.
- Drew significant criticism in 2024 for a post attacking city supervisors.

## Connections
- [[Y Combinator]], [[Paul Graham]], [[Sam Altman]].
- [[OpenAI]], [[Anthropic]] — the two labs YC startups build on.
