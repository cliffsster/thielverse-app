> [!abstract] David Gausebeck
> [[PayPal]]'s first technical hire after the founders; co-author of the Gausebeck–Levchin
> test and co-founder of Matterport.

## Key facts
- University of Illinois; joined [[Confinity]] as an early engineer.
- Co-designed the Gausebeck–Levchin test with [[Max Levchin]] — among the first commercial deployments of a CAPTCHA to block automated account creation, and a key part of PayPal's survival against fraud.
- Co-founded Matterport in 2011 and served as its chief scientist; the company went public in 2021.

## Connections
- [[Max Levchin]] — co-inventor and PayPal CTO.
- [[Nathan Gettings]] — fellow PayPal anti-fraud engineer whose work led to [[Palantir Technologies]].
- [[PayPal]] — origin.
