> [!abstract] Trae Stephens
> Early [[Palantir Technologies]] employee, partner at [[Founders Fund]], and co-founder and
> chairman of [[Anduril Industries]]. The clearest single link between the Thiel capital
> network and the new defence-hardware industry.

## Key facts
- Georgetown School of Foreign Service; worked in the US intelligence community before tech.
- At Palantir 2008–2014, deployed into intelligence and defence customers — where he formed the thesis that the same gap existed in hardware.
- Joined [[Founders Fund]] as a partner in 2014.
- Served on [[Donald Trump]]'s Department of Defense transition team in 2016, giving the network an early foothold in defence personnel decisions.
- Co-founded [[Anduril Industries]] in 2017 with [[Palmer Luckey]], [[Brian Schimpf]], [[Matt Grimm]] and Joe Chen; serves as chairman.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — Founders Fund partner.
- [[Palmer Luckey]] — Anduril co-founder; Stephens and [[Founders Fund]] backed him after his exit from Facebook.
- [[Brian Schimpf]], [[Matt Grimm]] — Palantir colleagues he recruited into Anduril.
- [[Christian Brose]] — Anduril chief strategy officer, ex-Senate Armed Services.
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — parallel Palantir-alumnus defence-tech investor.
- [[U.S. Department of Defense]] — 2016 transition team, then Anduril's principal customer.
