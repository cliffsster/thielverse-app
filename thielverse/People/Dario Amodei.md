> [!abstract] Dario Amodei
> Co-founder and CEO of [[Anthropic]]; formerly VP of Research at [[OpenAI]]. The most
> prominent voice arguing that this network's AI acceleration is dangerous — while selling
> to the same government customers.

## Key facts
- Princeton PhD in physics/computational neuroscience; Baidu and Google Brain before OpenAI.
- Led OpenAI's GPT-2 and GPT-3 work; left in 2020 with his sister [[Daniela Amodei]] and a group of researchers over disagreements about safety and commercialisation, founding [[Anthropic]] in 2021.
- Wrote *Machines of Loving Grace* (2024) and has repeatedly predicted powerful AI within a few years while pushing for regulation — the stance [[David Sacks]] publicly attacked in 2025 as regulatory capture.
- Under his leadership Anthropic partnered with [[Palantir Technologies]] and AWS in November 2024 to put Claude into US defence and intelligence environments — drawing criticism that Anthropic's safety positioning and its customer list conflict.

- [[Anthropic]] — co-founder and CEO.

- [[Marc Andreessen]] — the *Techno-Optimist Manifesto* is a direct rebuttal of his position.
