> [!abstract] Andrew McCormack
> [[PayPal]] operations staffer who became [[Peter Thiel]]'s assistant and later co-founded
> [[Valar Ventures]].

## Key facts
- Worked in PayPal operations and then directly for Thiel.
- Co-founded [[Valar Ventures]] with Thiel and James Fitzgerald in 2010, focused on fintech outside Silicon Valley — its best-known positions include N26, TransferWise/Wise and Xero.
- Ran restaurants in San Francisco in between, an outlier in this cohort.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — employer, then co-founder.
- [[Valar Ventures]] — his firm.
- [[Jack Selby]] — the other PayPal alum who worked most directly inside Thiel's own vehicles.
