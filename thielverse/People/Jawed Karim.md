> [!abstract] Jawed Karim
> [[PayPal]] engineer, third co-founder of [[YouTube]], and the person in its first uploaded
> video, *Me at the zoo*.

## Key facts
- Born 1979 in East Germany, raised in Germany and Minnesota; University of Illinois CS.
- Worked on core PayPal payments infrastructure and real-time anti-fraud systems.
- Co-founded [[YouTube]] in 2005 but stepped back early to study at Stanford, taking a smaller stake than [[Chad Hurley]] and [[Steve Chen]].
- Co-founded Youniversity Ventures, an early-stage fund that was among the small set of outside seed backers around the PayPal-alumni startup wave.

## Connections
- [[Chad Hurley]], [[Steve Chen]] — YouTube co-founders.
- [[Max Levchin]] — University of Illinois to PayPal pipeline.
- [[YouTube]], [[PayPal]].
