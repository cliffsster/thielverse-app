> [!abstract] Robert F. Kennedy Jr.
> Secretary of Health and Human Services since 2025. [[Jim O'Neill]] — two decades a [[Peter Thiel]] employee — is his deputy.

## Key facts
- Environmental lawyer turned vaccine critic; ran for president in 2024 as a Democrat, then an independent, before endorsing Trump.
- Confirmed to HHS in February 2025; launched the "Make America Healthy Again" agenda and replaced the CDC's vaccine advisory committee.
- Removed CDC director Susan Monarez in August 2025, after which O'Neill added acting CDC director to his deputy-secretary role — putting a Thiel associate atop the agency.
- His department is where the Thiel network's longevity and drug-approval views meet actual regulatory authority.
