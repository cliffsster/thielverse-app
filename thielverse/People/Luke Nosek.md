> [!abstract] Luke Nosek
> Co-founder of [[Confinity]]/[[PayPal]] and of [[Founders Fund]]; later founder of
> [[Gigafund]]. The person who turned the PayPal network into a durable capital institution.

## Key facts
- Born 1975 in Poland, raised in Illinois. University of Illinois CS, where he met [[Max Levchin]].
- Co-founded [[Confinity]] in 1998 with [[Peter Thiel]], Levchin and [[Ken Howery]]; ran marketing and product.
- Co-founded [[Founders Fund]] in 2005 with Thiel and Howery — the vehicle that institutionalised the PayPal Mafia.
- Championed and led Founders Fund's investment in [[SpaceX]], one of the best-performing venture positions ever made.
- Left Founders Fund in 2017 to start [[Gigafund]] with Stephen Oskoui, a fund heavily concentrated in SpaceX.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Ken Howery]] — Confinity and Founders Fund co-founders.
- [[Max Levchin]] — university friend who introduced him to Thiel.
- [[Elon Musk]] — the SpaceX bet that defined his career.
- [[Founders Fund]], [[Gigafund]], [[SpaceX]] — his institutional footprint.
