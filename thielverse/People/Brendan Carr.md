> [!abstract] Brendan Carr
> Chairman of the [[Federal Communications Commission]] since 2025; wrote the FCC chapter of Project 2025. The regulator whose approval the media consolidation in this vault runs through.

## Key facts
- FCC commissioner from 2017; elevated to chairman in January 2025.
- Authored the [[Heritage Foundation]]'s Project 2025 FCC chapter, which argued for reining in what it described as biased broadcasters and for reinterpreting Section 230.
- Opened proceedings against broadcast networks and made public statements about licences during the September 2025 suspension of Jimmy Kimmel's ABC show — widely read as regulatory pressure on programming decisions.
- The FCC signs off on broadcast-licence transfers, which puts him in the path of [[David Ellison]]'s [[Paramount Skydance]] transactions.
