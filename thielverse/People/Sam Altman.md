> [!abstract] Sam Altman
> CEO of [[OpenAI]] and former president of Y Combinator. Backed early by [[Peter Thiel]],
> and now on the opposite side of [[Elon Musk]] in the defining AI rivalry.

## Key facts
- Born 1985. Stanford dropout; founded Loopt, then ran Y Combinator from 2014 to 2019.
- Ran Hydrazine Capital, a fund whose largest backer was Thiel.
- Co-founded [[OpenAI]] in 2015 with [[Elon Musk]] and others; Thiel was among the earliest announced donors to the original nonprofit.
- Fired and reinstated as OpenAI CEO across five days in November 2023 — a board fight that included [[Reid Hoffman]]-era directors.
- Musk sued OpenAI over its conversion away from nonprofit control; the two are now open adversaries.
