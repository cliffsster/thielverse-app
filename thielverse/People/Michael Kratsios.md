> [!abstract] Michael Kratsios
> Chief of staff at Thiel Capital, then US Chief Technology Officer (2019–2021) and
> Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy from 2025.

## Key facts
- Born 1986. Princeton politics; principal and chief of staff to [[Peter Thiel]] at Thiel Capital.
- Joined the first Trump administration in 2017 as deputy CTO, became US CTO in 2019, and drove the American AI Initiative executive order and the federal AI R&D strategy.
- Also served as acting Under Secretary of Defense for Research and Engineering in 2020.
- Managing director at Scale AI between administrations.
- Confirmed as OSTP Director in 2025 and named [[Donald Trump]]'s science adviser — placing a Thiel-trained operative at the top of federal science and AI policy.
