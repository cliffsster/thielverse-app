> [!abstract] Steve Bannon
> Former Trump chief strategist and *War Room* host — and the most aggressive right-wing
> antagonist of this entire network, which he calls "techno-feudalist".

## Key facts
- Breitbart chairman, 2016 Trump campaign CEO, White House chief strategist for seven months in 2017.
- Served four months in prison in 2024 for contempt of Congress.
- Led the December 2024 revolt against [[Elon Musk]] and [[David Sacks]] over H-1B visas, framing the tech right as hostile to the MAGA base.
- Has attacked [[Peter Thiel]] and the transhumanist and life-extension strands of the network directly, and campaigned against [[Palantir Technologies]]-style data consolidation as a threat to civil liberties from the populist right.
- Useful as a graph node precisely because he shows the coalition this network entered is not stable.
