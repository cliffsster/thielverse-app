> [!abstract] Stephen Cohen
> Co-founder and President of [[Palantir Technologies]]. The quietest of the five founders and
> the one who built the first working product.

## Key facts
- Born c. 1982–83. Stanford computer science; met [[Joe Lonsdale]] there.
- Co-founded Palantir at roughly 22 and wrote much of the early prototype that turned [[PayPal]]'s fraud-detection approach into an intelligence-analysis tool.
- Became President of Palantir and a director; consistently among the largest individual shareholders after [[Peter Thiel]] and [[Alex Karp]].
- Almost never gives interviews — deliberately the lowest-profile founder.

## Connections
- [[Joe Lonsdale]] — Stanford classmate and co-founder.
- [[Alex Karp]] — long-running CEO/President operating partnership.
- [[Peter Thiel]], [[Nathan Gettings]] — co-founders.
- [[Shyam Sankar]] — the engineering leadership he handed the platform to.
- [[Palantir Technologies]] — Gotham and Foundry both descend from his early prototype work.
