> [!abstract] Curtis Yarvin
> Neoreactionary writer, formerly blogging as "Mencius Moldbug", and founder of the computing
> project Urbit — in which [[Peter Thiel]] was an investor.

## Key facts
- Software engineer turned political blogger; his "Unqualified Reservations" writing from 2007 onward argued that democratic government should be replaced by something closer to a corporate monarchy run by a CEO-sovereign.
- Founded Tlon, the company behind the Urbit decentralised computing platform; Thiel was an early investor. Yarvin later left the company.
- His ideas — particularly "RAGE" (Retire All Government Employees) and the notion of dismantling the civil service — circulate visibly in the 2025 administration's approach to federal staffing.
- [[JD Vance]] has cited him by name in interviews.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — investor in his company and the principal financial link between him and Silicon Valley.
- [[JD Vance]] — publicly acknowledged reader.
- [[Department of Government Efficiency]] — the clearest institutional echo of his civil-service arguments, though he holds no role in it.
- [[Eric Weinstein]] — adjacent Thiel-funded heterodox intellectual milieu.

## Also connected

- [[Balaji Srinivasan]] — parallel exit theorist, more technocratic than monarchist.
- [[Remilia Corporation]], [[Charlotte Fang]] — overlapping online-right cultural space, no organisational tie.
- [[Nick Fuentes]] — a different and mutually hostile strand of the online right.
