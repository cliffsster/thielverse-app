> [!abstract] Steve Chen
> [[PayPal]] engineer and co-founder and CTO of [[YouTube]].

## Key facts
- Born 1978 in Taipei, raised in Illinois; University of Illinois CS, where the [[Max Levchin]] recruiting pipeline ran.
- Early engineer at PayPal; briefly worked at Facebook before leaving to build YouTube.
- Co-founded [[YouTube]] in 2005 and built its video infrastructure; stayed at Google after the acquisition, later founding AVOS with [[Chad Hurley]].

## Connections
- [[Chad Hurley]], [[Jawed Karim]] — YouTube co-founders.
- [[Max Levchin]] — University of Illinois recruiting pipeline into PayPal.
- [[Roelof Botha]] — Sequoia seed investor.
