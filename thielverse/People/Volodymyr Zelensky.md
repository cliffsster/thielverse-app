> [!abstract] Volodymyr Zelensky
> President of Ukraine; leads the state that has become the most consequential proving ground for this network's defence technology.

## Key facts
- President since 2019; led Ukraine's defence following the 2022 Russian invasion.
- [[Palantir Technologies]] software has been used extensively in Ukrainian targeting and battlefield analysis; [[Alex Karp]] has said publicly that Palantir is responsible for a large share of Ukraine's targeting.
- Ukraine's communications depend heavily on [[Elon Musk]]'s Starlink, which has made Musk's decisions a matter of Ukrainian operational security.
- [[Eric Schmidt]]'s drone company Swift Beat supplies Ukrainian forces directly.
