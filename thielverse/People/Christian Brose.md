> [!abstract] Christian Brose
> Chief Strategy Officer at [[Anduril Industries]] and previously staff director of the Senate
> Armed Services Committee under John McCain — the network's most direct link into
> congressional defence policy.

## Key facts
- Speechwriter and policy adviser to Condoleezza Rice at the State Department, then senior adviser to Senator John McCain.
- Staff director of the Senate Armed Services Committee, 2015–2018, running the machinery that writes the annual defence authorisation bill.
- Wrote *The Kill Chain* (2020), arguing US defence procurement is structurally incapable of fielding modern autonomous systems — the intellectual case Anduril sells against.
- Joined Anduril in 2019 as head of strategy.
