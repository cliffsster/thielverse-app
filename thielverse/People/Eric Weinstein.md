> [!abstract] Eric Weinstein
> Longtime managing director of Thiel Capital and the man who coined the term
> "Intellectual Dark Web".

## Key facts
- Harvard PhD in mathematical physics; research fellow at Oxford before moving into finance.
- Managing director at Thiel Capital for roughly a decade, acting as an in-house intellectual and idea-generator rather than a conventional investor.
- Coined "Intellectual Dark Web" in 2018 to describe a loose set of heterodox public figures; hosts *The Portal*.
- Advances a "stagnation" thesis — that scientific and economic progress stalled around 1970 — that closely tracks [[Peter Thiel]]'s own public arguments.

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — employer and intellectual collaborator.
- [[Curtis Yarvin]] — adjacent in the Thiel-funded heterodox-intellectual space, though very different politics.
- [[Thiel Foundation]] — overlapping ideas about technological stagnation.
