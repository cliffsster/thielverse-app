> [!abstract] JD Vance
> Vice President of the United States since January 2025. Former [[Mithril Capital]] principal
> and founder of [[Narya Capital]] — the most senior office ever held by a direct
> [[Peter Thiel]] protégé.

## Key facts
- Born 1984 in Middletown, Ohio. Marine Corps, Ohio State, Yale Law (2013).
- Met [[Peter Thiel]] after a 2011 talk Thiel gave at Yale Law — Vance has repeatedly described it as the most important lecture of his life.
- Published *Hillbilly Elegy* in 2016, then worked at Mithril Capital (Thiel's fund with [[Ajay Royan]]) and at Steve Case's Revolution.
- Founded [[Narya Capital]] in Cincinnati in 2020, backed by Thiel, Marc Andreessen and [[Eric Schmidt]]. Narya invested in Rumble and in [[Anduril Industries]]-adjacent defence-tech deals.
- Thiel gave roughly $15m to the Protect Ohio Values super PAC supporting Vance's 2022 Senate primary — at the time the largest sum ever given to support a single Senate candidate.
- Elected US Senator for Ohio in 2022; chosen as [[Donald Trump]]'s running mate in July 2024; sworn in as Vice President in January 2025.
- Has publicly cited [[Curtis Yarvin]]'s writing in interviews, one of the more direct traces of Thiel-adjacent neoreactionary thought into elected office.
