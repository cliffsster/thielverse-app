> [!abstract] Larry Summers
> Former US Treasury Secretary and Harvard president whose long correspondence with [[Jeffrey Epstein]] cost him his [[OpenAI]] board seat in 2025 — the point where the Epstein story intersects this network directly.

## Key facts
- Treasury Secretary 1999–2001; Harvard president 2001–2006; director of the National Economic Council 2009–2010.
- Joined the [[OpenAI]] board in November 2023 as part of the reconstituted board after [[Sam Altman]]'s firing and reinstatement.
- In November 2025, House Oversight released estate correspondence showing his contact with Epstein continued well past the 2008 conviction and into 2019. Summers withdrew from public commitments and left the OpenAI board.
- His exit is the clearest case of the Epstein material producing a concrete governance consequence inside a company in this vault.
