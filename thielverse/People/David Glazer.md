> [!abstract] David Glazer
> Chief Financial Officer of [[Palantir Technologies]]; took the company through its 2020
> direct listing.

## Key facts
- Joined Palantir in 2013 from Goldman Sachs.
- Architected the September 2020 NYSE direct listing rather than a conventional IPO — a choice that let insiders including [[Peter Thiel]] retain control via a triple-class share structure.
- Oversaw the shift from cash-burning private company to consistent GAAP profitability from 2023 onward.
