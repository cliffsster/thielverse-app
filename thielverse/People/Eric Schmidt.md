> [!abstract] Eric Schmidt
> Former Google CEO and chairman; chaired the Defense Innovation Board and the
> [[National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence]]. Not a Thiel ally, but the
> other pole of the Silicon-Valley-meets-Pentagon story — and a [[Narya Capital]] backer.

## Key facts
- CEO of Google 2001–2011, executive chairman of Alphabet until 2017.
- Chaired the Pentagon's Defense Innovation Board (2016–2020) and the congressionally mandated [[National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence]] (2018–2021), whose final report drove much of current US AI-and-national-security policy.
- Backed [[JD Vance]]'s [[Narya Capital]] alongside [[Peter Thiel]] and Marc Andreessen — a rare direct financial link across the network's political divide.
- Runs Schmidt Futures and Schmidt Sciences; founded the defence-tech company White Stork/Swift Beat supplying drones to Ukraine.
- Became CEO of Relativity Space in 2025.
