> [!abstract] Mette Frederiksen
> Prime Minister of Denmark — the country [[Ken Howery]] was named US ambassador to, at a moment when the administration was pressing publicly for control of Greenland.

## Key facts
- Social Democrat, prime minister since 2019.
- Faced sustained pressure in 2025 over Greenland, a Danish autonomous territory, after [[Donald Trump]] repeatedly said the US should acquire it and declined to rule out coercion.
- Howery's ambassadorship — a [[PayPal]] co-founder and [[Founders Fund]] partner posted to Copenhagen — turned a routine diplomatic appointment into a live geopolitical assignment.
