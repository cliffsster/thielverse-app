> [!abstract] Nayib Bukele
> President of El Salvador; the tech right's favourite head of state, and the counterparty to the US deportation arrangements [[Stephen Miller]] negotiated.

## Key facts
- President since 2019; styles himself "the world's coolest dictator"; won re-election in 2024 after a constitutional reinterpretation.
- Made bitcoin legal tender in 2021, making him a hero to the crypto wing of this network.
- Accepted deportees from the US into the CECOT mega-prison under a 2025 agreement, including people later found to have been removed in error.
- His mass-incarceration security model is cited approvingly by parts of the American right as proof that state capacity beats proceduralism — the same argument [[Curtis Yarvin]] makes abstractly.

- [[Stephen Miller]], [[U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement]] — the deportation agreement.
- [[Donald Trump]] — the bilateral relationship.
- [[Curtis Yarvin]], [[Balaji Srinivasan]] — the ideological affinity, not an organisational tie.
