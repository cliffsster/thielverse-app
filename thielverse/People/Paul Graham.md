> [!abstract] Paul Graham
> Co-founder of [[Y Combinator]] and the person who installed [[Sam Altman]] as its president —
> then, years later, publicly complicated the story of how Altman left.

## Key facts
- Co-founded Viaweb (sold to Yahoo, 1998), then [[Y Combinator]] in 2005 with Jessica Livingston, Trevor Blackwell and Robert Morris.
- Funded Altman's Loopt in YC's first batch and later handed him the presidency in 2014.
- Stated in 2024 that Altman was asked to choose between running YC and running [[OpenAI]] — contradicting the tidier public version of that transition.
- Politically a liberal and a persistent critic of the tech right's turn; an outlier among people this central to Silicon Valley's institutions.

## Connections
- [[Sam Altman]] — mentor, then successor, then awkward public accounting.
- [[Y Combinator]], [[Garry Tan]].
- [[Peter Thiel]] — Thiel was briefly a YC part-time partner under Altman; the two men's politics diverge sharply.
