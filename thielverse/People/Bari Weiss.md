> [!abstract] Bari Weiss
> Founder of [[The Free Press]], co-founder of the [[University of Austin]] with [[Joe Lonsdale]], and Editor-in-Chief of CBS News since 2025 — the network's most direct line into a legacy broadcast newsroom.

## Key facts
- Resigned as a *New York Times* opinion editor in 2020 with a public letter about intolerance of heterodox views; founded The Free Press on Substack.
- Co-founded the [[University of Austin]] in 2021 alongside Lonsdale, Niall Ferguson and Pano Kanelos.
- [[Paramount Skydance]] acquired The Free Press in October 2025 for a reported ~$150m and installed her as Editor-in-Chief of CBS News — an extraordinary jump from an independent newsletter to running a legacy network news division.
- That appointment is the single clearest instance of this network's media ecosystem merging with mainstream broadcast media.
