> [!abstract] Sean Parker
> Napster co-founder, Facebook's first president, and a managing partner at [[Founders Fund]] —
> the person who introduced [[Peter Thiel]] to [[Mark Zuckerberg]].

## Key facts
- Born 1979. Co-founded Napster at 19 with Shawn Fanning, then Plaxo.
- Became Facebook's founding president in 2004 and brought Zuckerberg to Thiel, producing the $500k investment that anchored Thiel's fortune.
- Managing partner at [[Founders Fund]] from 2006 to 2014.
- Founded the Parker Institute for Cancer Immunotherapy in 2016 with a $250m gift, and Brigade, a civic-engagement app.

## Connections
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]] — recruited Thiel as first outside investor.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — Founders Fund partner and the beneficiary of his Facebook introduction.
- [[Founders Fund]], [[Meta Platforms]].
- [[Reid Hoffman]] — the other early Facebook angel from the PayPal circle.
