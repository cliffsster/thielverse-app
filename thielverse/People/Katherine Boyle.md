> [!abstract] Katherine Boyle
> General partner at [[Andreessen Horowitz]] and co-founder of its American Dynamism practice —
> the person who gave the defence-tech thesis its name and its lobbying vocabulary.

## Key facts
- Former *Washington Post* reporter; joined General Catalyst, then a16z in 2021.
- Launched American Dynamism in 2022: investing in companies serving the national interest — defence, aerospace, housing, education, manufacturing.
- Portfolio and thesis overlap heavily with [[Founders Fund]]'s: [[Anduril Industries]], [[Flock Safety]], Shield AI, Saronic, Hadrian, Apex.
- Runs the annual American Dynamism Summit in Washington, which functions as the sector's main political gathering.
