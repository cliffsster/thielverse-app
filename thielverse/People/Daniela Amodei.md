> [!abstract] Daniela Amodei
> Co-founder and President of [[Anthropic]]; previously VP of Operations at [[OpenAI]].

## Key facts
- Background in international development and operations; ran people and operations at Stripe before OpenAI.
- Co-founded [[Anthropic]] in 2021 with her brother [[Dario Amodei]] and five other OpenAI researchers.
- Built Anthropic's Public Benefit Corporation structure and its Long-Term Benefit Trust — a governance design meant to survive commercial pressure, and a deliberate contrast to the OpenAI board structure that failed in 2023.
