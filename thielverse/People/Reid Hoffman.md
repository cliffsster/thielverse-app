> [!abstract] Reid Hoffman
> [[PayPal]] COO/EVP, founder of [[LinkedIn]], partner at [[Greylock Partners]], and the
> single largest Democratic donor in the PayPal network — [[Peter Thiel]]'s closest friend
> and clearest political opposite.

## Key facts
- Born 1967. Stanford symbolic systems (where he met Thiel), Oxford MPhil philosophy on a Marshall Scholarship.
- Joined [[PayPal]] as COO and ran business development, regulatory and payments partnerships — the non-engineering half of surviving as a payments company.
- Founded [[LinkedIn]] in 2003; Microsoft bought it for $26.2B in 2016. Sits on the Microsoft board.
- Partner at [[Greylock Partners]] since 2009; early investor in Facebook ([[Meta Platforms]]), Airbnb and Zynga.
- Co-founded Inflection AI (2022) and sat on the [[OpenAI]] board until 2023.
- Major Democratic funder and vocal Trump critic; funded litigation against Trump, drawing direct public conflict with Thiel and [[David Sacks]].

## Connections
- [[Peter Thiel]] — Stanford friends since the 1980s; they debate publicly and remain close.
- [[Elon Musk]], [[Max Levchin]], [[David Sacks]], [[Keith Rabois]] — PayPal colleagues now mostly on the other side of his politics.
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]] — early Facebook investor alongside Thiel.
- [[Sam Altman]] — OpenAI board colleague.
- [[Greylock Partners]], [[LinkedIn]] — his institutional bases.
