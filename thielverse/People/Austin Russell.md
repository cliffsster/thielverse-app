> [!abstract] Austin Russell
> Founder of Luminar Technologies and a 2013 [[Thiel Fellowship]] recipient; briefly the
> world's youngest self-made billionaire.

## Key facts
- Left Stanford at 17 on a Thiel Fellowship to build lidar for autonomous vehicles.
- Founded Luminar in 2012; it went public via SPAC in 2020, briefly valuing his stake above $2B.
- Attempted to acquire *Forbes* in 2023 — the deal collapsed. Luminar's valuation fell sharply from its peak and he stepped down as CEO in 2025.
