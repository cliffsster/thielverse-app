> [!abstract] Palmer Luckey
> Founder of Oculus, sold to Facebook for ~$2B and then fired from it — and founder of
> [[Anduril Industries]], now one of the largest defence-technology companies in the US.

## Key facts
- Born 1992 in Long Beach, California; built Oculus Rift prototypes as a teenager and Kickstarted it in 2012.
- Facebook ([[Meta Platforms]]) acquired Oculus in 2014; Luckey was dismissed in 2017 after reporting on his $10,000 donation to a pro-Trump group called Nimble America. He later received a settlement.
- Founded [[Anduril Industries]] in 2017 with [[Trae Stephens]], [[Brian Schimpf]], [[Matt Grimm]] and Joe Chen, backed by [[Founders Fund]].
- Anduril builds Lattice (its software layer), Sentry surveillance towers on the US southern border, counter-drone systems, Ghost and Bolt drones, Fury autonomous fighters, and took over Microsoft's IVAS Army headset programme in 2025.
- A significant Republican donor and one of the loudest public advocates for Silicon Valley working with the Pentagon — the position [[Palantir Technologies]] pioneered.

## Connections
- [[Trae Stephens]] — Palantir alum, Founders Fund partner, and his co-founder and chairman.
- [[Brian Schimpf]], [[Matt Grimm]] — Palantir alumni who run Anduril operationally.
- [[Mark Zuckerberg]] — acquirer, then employer, then the firing that made Anduril happen.
- [[Peter Thiel]] — [[Founders Fund]] as Anduril's foundational backer.
