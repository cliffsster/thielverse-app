> [!abstract] Premal Shah
> [[PayPal]] product manager who left to become founding president of Kiva, the microlending
> nonprofit — the clearest counter-example to the network's usual trajectory.

## Key facts
- Stanford economics; product manager at PayPal in the early 2000s.
- Joined Kiva in 2006 as founding president and scaled it to more than $1B in loans across 80+ countries.
- Applied PayPal's payments and trust infrastructure thinking to nonprofit microfinance.
